# A builder asks the orchestrator mid-build, and only what it defers reaches the owner

A builder can ask the board's session, the orchestrator, a question at any point while it builds, and carries on meanwhile. The owner never answers a builder mid-build: the orchestrator answers, or defers the question to the owner, who sees it at review. This supersedes the questions-up-front step of [a builder records its judgment calls and the session verifies them before land](2026-11-03-0514-a-builder-records-its-judgment-calls-and-the-session-verifies-them.md) for anything the builder can ask while it works; the judgment calls and their verification stay.

**The ask is a thread line and an event.** `board.mjs builder ask <key> "<question>"` needs `--site` and the agent's open record. It writes a thread line `{kind: builder-ask, ask: ask<n>, agent}` with `who` the builder's model, "Claude Opus builder", and appends `builder-ask {key, ask, agent, text}` to the board's events, which wakes the session (`bin/wait-events.mjs` prints "ask1 from #139's builder: …"; usage pauses don't hold it, since the builder is running). Ids count the card's asks in its thread. `builder asks <key>` prints each with its answer, its deferral or "waiting on the orchestrator", which is how the builder reads the reply.

**The answer or deferral is the orchestrator's line.** `builder answer <key> <ask> --text` and `builder defer <key> <ask> --text` write `{kind: builder-answer, ask, deferred?}` as claude. The thread names every claude line "Orchestrator" instead of "Claude", replies included (`board/public/ui-threads.js`); an ask can be answered or deferred once.

**The card stays quiet.** Neither line counts as unread (`board/domain.mjs` `countsUnread`, `ui-threads.js` `unread`), queues a push, or answers an owner message waiting in the thread (`claudeAtOf`). The card's status, lane and chips don't change while the orchestrator handles it (owner's pick C on `gh139-ask-flow`, `public/prototypes/builder-ask-3/`).

**A deferral goes to the owner after review.** `builder defer` also appends `Question: <ask> (deferred to the owner)` to the asking agent's record's calls, so the build hands back to checking with Next "Answer the builder's questions" and `land` waits for `builder verified`. After its other checks, and before a draft PR or In Review, the session puts it to the owner as a card question naming the ask (`"ask": "ask<n>"` in `question add`, which refuses an ask that wasn't deferred). `builder verified` refuses while a deferred ask from that agent has no such question (`board/store.mjs` `verifyCalls`).

**A deferred question shows as new, with no Action needed.** A card's open questions asked since this browser last opened it count as new, ahead of its new messages in the same chip: "1 new question · 1 new message" (`board/public/ui-cards.js` `timeLine`, `app-cards.js`). This holds for every card question. A card whose only open questions are deferred asks waits on the owner from where it is, In Progress, with the Open questions button, Next "Answer 1 question" and no Action needed (`board/domain.mjs` `placeOwnerWait`); any other open question brings Action needed as before.

The owner, on `gh139-ask-look`: "these should not block on me. They should go straight to the orchestrator. And if the orchestrator cannot confidently answer them or still wants my opinion, it gets flagged as a judgment call to review in post. as open questions that are added after the orchestrator reviews everything else, but before a draft PR is sent out before it moves to in review for sure", "maybe we can have that in the thread as normal messages, just as long as the identifier is like Claude Opus builder. And maybe the ones that are the orchestrator, instead of just saying Claude, they should say orchestrator", and "new questions, then new messages". Picked take C, "card stays quiet", over A (a "builder asked" status line) and B (A plus Action needed).

Not in this change, from the same card (#139): the hub launching the builder headless from the Implement tap, ready refusing without a size and complete notes, and land never going to the orchestrator. Those touch the hub's process launching and the land path, and each stands on its own.

Considered:
- the builder blocking until the orchestrator replies: a sleeping orchestrator would stall every build, and the owner asked that nothing block;
- a `who: orchestrator` author on the session's lines: every existing line is claude, so naming claude "Orchestrator" covers them all at once;
- counting the orchestrator's answer as a new message: take A, which the owner passed over for C;
- linking the owner's question to the ask by time instead of an `ask` field: a later unrelated question would count as putting the ask to the owner.
