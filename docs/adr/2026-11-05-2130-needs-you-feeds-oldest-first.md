# Needs you feeds oldest first

Needs you lists the line that has waited longest first, to match Todo's oldest-on-top order. The deck steps through the same order, since it walks the lines. Pending prompts still lead, each holding a session, and held reviews, which carry no time, follow the rest.

- **Oldest** means `since` on the line (`domain.mjs` `needsOf`): its card's `created`, the key Todo sorts by, for question, Ready and Action needed lines alike. The owner, on #310, after it landed: "this should sort based on the created at of the related work item, not the order of arrival of questions" (#313). A topic with no card (`lineup:`, `wave:` and ask topics) has no `created`; it takes its oldest open question's `asked_at`, the first moment it waited.
- **A line with no stamp** comes after the stamped ones, in board order.
- **Waiting on you's topic pills follow the same order** (`domain.mjs` sorts `ownerTopics` by each topic's Needs you line; topics with no open question keep their order after), so the phone's deck, the desktop pane's lines and the pills on Waiting on you, embedded or on its own page, read alike.
- **From 900px, the pane's orange Needs you leaves out a line that only asks questions** (`app-needs.js`), since Waiting on you under it shows them; prompts, Ready on GitHub, Action needed and held reviews stay, and with none of those the orange section is gone. The test is the stylesheet's split width, `(min-width: 900px)`. A phone keeps its deck at the top. The owner, on #310: "on desktop where the needs you is the right side, don't also show the needs you orange section at the top". Hiding the whole section was set aside: on a desktop it is the only place a board's pending prompts are answered.

Before this, topic lines came first, then card lines in board order, newest first. #310 first sorted card lines by their oldest open question, else their review start or last update; #313 replaced that with the card's created time.

Then #332: lines of cards in the In Progress lane (`lane === 'progress'`) come before every other card's, each group keeping the created-time order (`domain.mjs` `oldestFirst`). Their Waiting on you pills carry `progress` and get the notice orange border (`--color-notice`, the In Progress orange). The owner: "Open questions from in progress should be shown topmost, then in order within that group based on created at ascending (oldest first). These items should have orange borders in the pills".

Then #829: the ? page's queue, and so Needs you, built from the same lines, takes the board's own order. The owner: "i want it sorted by age of card, it should match the board." Card lines keep the order `laneOrder` (`public/ui-order.js`) gives the items: In Review, then In Progress, then Todo, bumped cards leading their lane, Todo oldest first and the other lanes newest first. A topic with no card follows the card lines, its oldest open question first. This replaces the created-time sort and the In Progress-first rule above; `since` on a line is still the card's created time, which the row's age reads.
