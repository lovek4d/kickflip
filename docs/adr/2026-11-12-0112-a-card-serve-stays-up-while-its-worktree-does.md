# A card's serve stays up while its worktree does

An In Review card's Visit went missing with no word (hub/T-95). `tick` in `hub/try.mjs` stopped a ready card serve after 30 minutes with no traffic through its relay and deleted its `data/try.json` entry. Auto serve brought it back only once the head moved, and with Auto serve on the card drew no Serve either, so the card had no link and no way to start one. The owner's direction: "the serve should always be present as long as a worktree is present. visit should only be available when serve has run successfully." This replaces the idle limit in [a try build serves on its own Tailscale port](2026-11-01-2254-a-try-build-serves-on-its-own-tailscale-port.md) and, for a ready card, the shutdown clearing in [a build the hub stops starts again after it restarts](2026-11-02-1731-a-build-the-hub-stops-starts-again-after-it-restarts.md).

- **No idle stop.** A card's serve runs while its card is open and its worktree is there. The relay no longer tracks visits.
- **The card's end stops it.** Each tick stops a card's run and drops its entry once the card is Done, all its PRs are merged or closed, it is gone from the board, or `installWorktree` finds no worktree, as after a land or a drop. A card the last hub left running whose card has ended is dropped the same way and doesn't start again.
- **A hub restart serves it again.** Shutdown keeps a ready card's `ready`. The next hub reads a web board's ready card as `running`, an interrupted build, and starts it at its next tick whatever the Auto serve setting. Its badge reads Serving… until it answers, then Visit. A hub that died hard leaves the same record.
- **An exit serves it again, three times in ten minutes.** A card's serve that was up and exits on its own, with any code or signal, starts again in the same worktree on the same port. The new log's first line says so, and the thread gets no line. A fourth exit within 10 minutes is a failure: the usual Serve failed line says it exited 4 times within 10 minutes. Retry starts with a clean count.
- **Maximum parallel serves.** A board setting, `max_serves` (1 to 99, 10 by default), in Board Settings under Maximum parallel planners: "Each open card worktree keeps one serve up. Installs aren’t counted. A busy host asks before it starts another." The hub counts the board's card runs, running or up. Main, the prototype site and an app board's builds don't count, and serving a card again takes no new slot.
- **Serve asks first at the cap or on a busy host.** The owner's Serve on a board at its cap is refused with a dialog: "This board is at its cap of N parallel serves. Kill another serve, or raise Maximum parallel serves in Board settings." While the host is busy ([a busy Mac holds new work](2026-11-06-0106-a-busy-mac-holds-new-work.md)), Serve's confirm reads "The host is busy, start this serve anyway?" and sends `anyway`. The hub refuses a busy-host serve without it, which the tap answers with the same question. Auto serve starts no new serve at the cap or on a busy host and starts it at a later tick. A restart's re-serve and an exit's re-serve skip both checks, since each takes back a slot it held.
- **Kill.** While a website card's serve is running or up, its Serve button reads Kill, Auto serve on or off. After a confirm, `POST /api/try/kill` reaches the hub's loopback `/_hub/kill`, which stops the run and frees its port and slot. The entry keeps the killed run's commit and `killed`, also through a hub restart, so Auto serve leaves the card until its head moves or the owner taps Serve. A killed serve is no exit, so it doesn't restart.
- **Visit only once served** stays as it was: the badge shows Visit only for a `ready` entry with a port.

Considered:
- Visit wakes an idle-stopped serve through a hub Starting page: the owner wants the serve up, not woken on demand.
- Keeping serves up only for cards with an open PR: a card in progress needs its serve as much as one in review.
- Queueing serves past the cap, or asking on the card itself: the owner picked a dialog on the Serve tap (prototype round serve-cap, take A).
