# A thread's questions open in a sheet from their line

The owner (T-196): "integrate questions into threads … when questions are posted, you can just click that in the thread … that opens up a modal with only that topic's questions, like the notes modal in that style. And then when you submit those questions, that hides. And at least on mobile, you still stay where you were on the page." Take A of the prototype won, then the owner corrected its words: "I don't want to change the text of it. Towards the nature of this thread being append only".

- **The line keeps its words.** A Questions added line still reads "N questions added", as posted ([a Questions added line is stored and stays in the thread](2026-11-13-0130-a-questions-added-line-is-stored-and-stays-in-the-thread.md)). It stays orange while a question of its batch is open and turns gray, not blue, once none is. Its tap opens the sheet (`data-questions`, its first question); the link to the questions page stays under it.
- **The sheet.** `app-questions-sheet.js` is the reader sheet's shape (`app-reader.js`): modal below 900px, over the pane and not modal from it, closed by its ✕, Escape, the dim area, a swipe down and Back. It lists only that card's open questions, drawn by `BoardUI.questionCard` as the questions page draws them, grouped by the batch whose line named them, newest batch first (a heading only when there are two or more). A question no line names comes last.
- **One answer path.** The pending test, the answer's shape and the draft handlers (pick, clear a pick, note, ⌘↵) moved out of `view-questions.js` into `ui-questions.js` (`pendingAnswers`, `answerOf`, `wireAnswerForm`), so the sheet and the page cannot drift. Send sends `owner-answers`, clears the drafts, closes the sheet and toasts; the page is not scrolled, so a phone stays where it was. The sheet draws no prototype eye: asking for a prototype stays on the questions page.
- **A push opens the card with the sheet.** A push tap (`from=push`) on a question of a card opens the board with that card selected and its sheet open: from 900px `focusQuestion(id, {sheet: true})` (`shell.js` asks it for a push), on a phone `view-questions.js` replaces the page with `#selectedCard=<key>&questions=<id>`, which the board reads once and spends (`router.js` `fragmentOf`). Every other way to a question, the header's ?, a topic's link, a card's Answer button, still shows the questions page.
- **Prototype questions stay on the page.** A prototype's question is answered in its viewer, so the sheet leaves it out and a push for one still shows the page.

The Waiting on you pane's overlay list (`app-pane.js`) now includes the sheet, so picking a topic in the pane closes it.

Supersedes the "blue once none has" point of [a Questions added line is stored and stays in the thread](2026-11-13-0130-a-questions-added-line-is-stored-and-stays-in-the-thread.md): it falls back to gray.
