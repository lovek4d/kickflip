# One loading screen until the board first paints

Card #942 (T-356, split from T-110). The owner chose take A of the round 1 prototype and A2 of the slow-line round, with the note "just host is slow to answer at 5 secs, no other state, keeps retrying".

Before this, a board with no saved copy showed empty lanes and an empty desktop pane until /api/board answered, and a load that timed out after 20 s, or that nothing answered, showed the down landing's "Board offline".

- **One screen over both panes.** From mount until the board's first render, the board view's root carries `loading`, and `#boardLoading` covers the lanes and the desktop Waiting on you pane together: the hub moon, pulsing, the board's name, and "Loading board..." under it, the owner's words. The pane needs no signal of its own: it holds no board of its own and paints in the same render as the lanes (`renderPane`, [the desktop pane shows the lanes' board](2026-11-14-2230-the-desktop-pane-shows-the-lanes-board.md)), so that render is when both have painted. The header stays, so the sidebar and header controls work while it shows. The banners above the lanes (start, pause, setup) are under it too.
- **The saved copy lifts it.** A copy of this build paints at once and the screen goes with that paint; the catch-up's changes then flash as before ([the board syncs by change number](2026-11-05-1819-the-board-syncs-by-change-number.md)).
- **From 5 s, one muted line.** "The host is slow to answer." sits under "Loading board...". There is no later line and no give-up.
- **It keeps retrying.** A load that times out (the 20 s abort) or that nothing answers (`downOf`'s unreachable) leaves the screen up, and the 5 s retry runs on. The down landing replaces the screen only when the hub says the board is down (restarting, stopped, removed, access, erroring), or when a board throws while drawing. Once the screen has gone, an unanswered load behaves as [a board out of reach keeps its copy or shows a landing](2026-11-06-0135-a-board-out-of-reach-keeps-its-copy-or-shows-a-landing.md) says, and that ADR's other-build copy still paints first when a load fails.
- **Considered:** skeleton lanes, and the sidebar's board row pulsing with the lanes blank (round 1 takes B and C); a slow line that replaces "Loading board..." and one that names the 20 s give-up (round 2 takes A1 and the 15 s line); quickening the moon instead of words (A3).
