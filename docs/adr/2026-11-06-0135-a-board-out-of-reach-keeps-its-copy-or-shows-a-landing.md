# A board out of reach keeps its copy, undimmed, or shows a landing

Superseded in part by [owner taps wait for the host in the outbox](2026-11-16-1600-owner-taps-wait-for-the-host-in-the-outbox.md): no button is held offline. Every tap waits in the outbox as "waiting for the host" and goes out once the board is back, so the **Buttons that send wait** bullet and Waiting on you's held sends no longer apply.

Before this, a failed board load dimmed the board to .72, turned off taps on everything but refresh, hid the composer, turned off the settings gear and showed one toast, "Offline. Use refresh in the header to retry." A reload during a restart usually found no copy, because a restart follows a land or an upgrade and the copy only paints under the build that saved it. The board then showed nothing, while the desktop right pane, Notes and Settings said "Board restarting…" for about 30 s and then "Offline. Is the host awake?". The owner asked for a landing in the style of the no-access page, and asked why the page doesn't just keep showing its local copy (#321; the grills and answers are in the card's notes).

- **The copy stays, undimmed (owner, gh321-offline-look B).** Offline, the board stays as it was, and cards, threads and folds open as before. Only the header time turns red. The dimming, the blocked taps and the toast are gone. The settings gear stays on, since Settings opens its copy (below).
- **Buttons that send wait (owner, gh321-usable A, gh321-buttons).** Drafts can be typed and stay per box. `ui.js` `SENDS` lists the buttons that send. While the board is out of reach they are greyed, and `holdSends` stops their taps and every form submit, Enter's included. The labelled ones read "<label> · Waits for the board", and icon buttons are only greyed. Edit, Notes, Visit, folds and other read controls stay live. Taps still wait for the server ([a tap waits for the server to record it](2026-11-03-1850-a-tap-waits-for-the-server-to-record-it.md)); queued offline taps are #220.
- **A landing only when there's no copy (gh321-landing-when A, gh321-landing-look).** With nothing to paint, the view shows `downLanding`: the kickflip mark, one bold line and one muted hint, the look of `hub/front-door.mjs`'s no-access page.
- **Six states (owner, gh321-states, "as written").** `ui.js` `downOf` reads each failed answer:

  | state | what the page sees | line | hint |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | restarting | `x-kickflip-down: code`, `starting` or `crashed` | Board restarting | Its code changed. / It's starting. / It stopped, and the hub is starting it again. Then: This page comes back by itself. |
  | stopped | `x-kickflip-down: stopped` (a `serves: port` board nothing answers for) | Board stopped | Nothing answers on its port. Its Claude session has to start it. |
  | removed | `x-kickflip-down: removed` (the hub's 404) | This board is gone | The hub no longer lists it. |
  | access | `x-kickflip-down: access` (a refusal), or the shell a dropped share redirects to | You no longer have access | Ask its owner to share it again. |
  | erroring | an answer the hub proxied (`x-kickflip-hub`) that failed or isn't JSON | Board restarting | It answers with errors. Retrying for 30 seconds. |
  | unreachable | nothing answered, the 20 s load timeout, `navigator.onLine` false, or an answer the hub never marked (Tailscale, with the hub down) | Board offline | The hub, your connection or the Mac is down. Retrying. |

  The hub marks its down page, its 404 and its refusal with `x-kickflip-down`, and every proxied answer with `x-kickflip-hub`. A 502 body has no other marker, so before this the page couldn't tell state 1 from state 2, or the hub being down from a board error. The board view's landing retries every 5 s, except for removed and access, which nothing brings back by itself. A board load now gives up after 20 s, so an asleep host reaches the red time or the landing rather than hanging.
- **An older build's copy, offline only (gh321-old-build A).** With nothing on screen after a failed load, the board view reads the copy whatever build saved it and paints it inside a try. If the paint throws, the view clears and shows the landing. Once the board answers, the next load asks for the whole board, not a patch from the old copy's number. Online, a copy from another build still reads as none ([the board syncs by change number](2026-11-05-1819-the-board-syncs-by-change-number.md)).
- **Waiting on you, Notes and Settings follow the same rule (gh321-surfaces A, gh321-copy-more "both, settings read-only").** At the first failed load, Waiting on you paints the board's own copy, live with its sends held. Notes keep each item's last answer in IndexedDB's new `pages` store (database `kickflip-boards`, version 2) and show it under "Copy of the notes as of <age>. Read only." Settings keeps every answer its parts read, from the board and the hub. When a part's load fails it paints from its copy, the body turns `inert` under "Copy of Settings as of <age>. Read only.", and the view reopens live once `api/settings` answers. A part with no copy waits out a restart for about 30 s as before, then the body shows the landing. These copies are only shown while the board is out of reach, under a line saying they are copies, so a builder's edit to a notes file never shows as current.
- **Considered:** a quiet strip with the as-of time (gh321-offline-look A; the owner chose the red time alone); keeping the dimming but letting cards open; waiting a full 30 s before Settings shows its copy, which would make an offline open wait; a landing even when a copy exists, with a link to it.
