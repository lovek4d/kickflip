# A closed card owes no brief

An owed brief used to stay an orchestrator wait in every lane, Done included, so a card closed as split with a prototype brief owed (T-566) kept 'brief T-566' on the waits for days; writing the brief would have launched a planner on a closed card.

A card in Done owes no brief: the waits ignore any its record still carries (domain.mjs orchestratorWaitsOf), and a move to Done drops the unwritten ones (cards.mjs placeIn). Written briefs stay for their launch.
