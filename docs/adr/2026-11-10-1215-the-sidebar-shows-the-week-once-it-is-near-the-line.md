# The sidebar shows the week once it is near the line

The Hub row's usage showed only the 5-hour bars, so the owner could not see the week closing in until it paused work.

Claude's live week now draws as a second "Claude week" bar under its 5-hour bar once it reaches the gate's near band, twice the buffer under the pause line (80% for the 90% line, following `pauseAt`, not a fixed 80). It is orange under the line and red at it; with the line Off there is no band, and a stale or reset week never shows. The owner chose this over replacing the 5-hour bar or a chip on the name line.
