# One human blue

The planner blue from [planners and implementers are two kinds of agent](2026-11-03-1551-planners-and-implementers-are-two-kinds-of-agent.md) is now the board's only blue. `--accent` takes its value, #2b6cb0 light and #6fa8e8 dark, so the new-messages count, links, primary buttons, focus rings, selected states, the prototype-requested badge (`--color-info`, still `var(--accent)`) and a planner's lines are one colour. `--accent-soft` takes the owner's bubble tint, #eef6ff light and #1b2533 dark, for the owner's bubble, a question's answer, selected backgrounds and the focus halo.

- **Fewer tokens.** `--love` and `--love-ink` are gone: with one blue they read the same as `--accent-soft` and `--accent`, so the owner's bubble and a planner's line use those.
- **The label on a fill.** The dark blue is light, so white on it is 2.49:1. `--on-accent` is the label on an accent fill: white in light (5.42:1), near-black #0b0b0e in dark (7.90:1). The primary button, the hot pill, the compose button, the sidebar's Go button and the hub's button use it. The decorative icon tiles keep white on their own colours.
- **Contrast.** The new-messages count on a card is 5.42:1 light and 7.12:1 dark.

The owner (hub/GH-147, 2026-11-03): "Would like to consolidate the color used for human blue. Like prototype blue seems to be different from new messages blue." Picked take B of the one-blue prototype (question `gh147-one-blue`).

Considered:
- the old accent (#5e6ad2 / #8c95f0) everywhere: least change, but the owner liked the planner blue more, and the white label in dark stays at 2.74:1;
- a new blue (#2c5fd0 / #7aa2f7): the only take passing 4.5:1 everywhere with no label change, but a new colour nobody had seen;
- keeping `--love` and `--love-ink` as aliases of the accent: two names for one colour, which is what made the blues drift apart.
