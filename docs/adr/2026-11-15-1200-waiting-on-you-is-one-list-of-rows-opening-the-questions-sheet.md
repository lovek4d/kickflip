# Waiting on you is one list of rows, each opening the questions sheet

#761 (T-413). Waiting on you held two shapes: topic pills over the open topic's question form, and tap rows beside them. The owner asked for one list of rows, one per thing waiting on them, every kind in one grammar, each opening its place. Prototype take A won: dense one-line rows, the key in mono, the title, the action owed at the right in its colour. The owner: "Text should wrap (within the column), but this is good." The ? badge counts rows, not questions.

- **One list.** `view-questions.js` draws `BoardUI.queueRows` as rows, in the board's order (oldest first, In Progress ahead), with an urgent question's topic first, under "N need you". Each row is `[key] [title] [action]` on a 56px / 1fr / auto grid: the title wraps within its column and the key and action stay at the top. The phone's page and the desktop pane draw the same rows. With none the page reads "Nothing is waiting on you" and "Answers, picks and taps land here."
- **The words and colours.** A card's topic is its ref and title, a page topic's key is Q with the topic's title. It reads Answer N, or Pick a take when each open question is a prototype pick. A tap reads its action's words (Tap Implement, Tap Close, Hold Run, a fault's reason). A held review is keyed PR and reads Review held. The action is orange, red on a fault or an urgent question, blue for a held review (AGENTS.md Colour priority).
- **A row opens its place.** A topic's row opens the questions sheet with that topic's open questions; a Pick a take opens its prototype; a held review opens in the Auto Review lane (`#review=N`); any other tap opens its card's thread. Closing returns to the list. In the desktop pane a topic's row also focuses its card on the board under the sheet, and the sheet closes the reader and the prototype viewer over the pane.
- **One sheet.** `app-questions-sheet.js` is now a factory, `BoardQuestionsSheet(dialog, deps)`. The board makes one over the lanes and the pane (a thread's questions-added line, a question link, a pane row). The phone's Waiting on you page makes its own in its markup, so closing it shows the list under it. The sheet draws every open question of the topic, prototype questions too with their View prototypes link, a page topic's as well as a card's, and each question keeps the thread's eye, fire, image and attach (`prototype-request`, `grill-more`, `visual-request`). Send answers with one `owner-answers`, toasts and closes.
- **A link to a question.** A link or push to an open question opens its sheet: on a phone over the list, from 900px over the board's pane (`focusQuestion`). A link to a topic, or to an answered question, marks its row.
- **Held reviews are in the queue.** `domain.mjs` `queueOf` keeps a held review's Needs you line as `{kind: 'review', review, title}`, after the cards, so the badge counts it too.

Dropped with the pills: the topic form and its move-on after Send (`nextQuestion`), the resolved fold and reopening a sent answer from the page, the urgent question shown alone (its row is red and first instead), Implement and Done tapped in place (the row opens the thread, where the tap is), the sent notice carried to the board, the moon, and the board's `questions=` fragment the phone's push redirect wrote.
