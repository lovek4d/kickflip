# A tag chip narrows the board under its own banner

Tapping a tag chip on a card narrows every lane to the cards carrying that tag, under its own banner ("Tag: <name> · N cards" with a ×) that stacks under the chain's and over the filter's. The chain, the tag, the filter and the search all intersect, each ended by its own ×.

- One tag at a time: a tap on another chip moves the banner, never stacks (as a chain re-roots).
- Names differing only in case are one tag, as the picker treats them.
- The tap does not open the card. Esc ends the search, then the chain, then the tag.
- Like the chain, it narrows the cards the page holds (the board's, Done's loaded pages and the search's matches); Done's Show more stops while a tag is on.
- In memory only, so a reload ends it. The "+N" chip and the open card's whole chips don't filter.
