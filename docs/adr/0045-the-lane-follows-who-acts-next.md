# The lane follows who acts next, and a card waiting on the owner says Action needed

A card's lane answers one question: who acts next? `board/domain.mjs` decides it in two steps. `laneOf` places the card by what Claude and GitHub say, and `placeOwnerWait` moves a card only the owner can move on to In Review and gives it its action.

- **In Progress: Claude or a builder is working.** That covers a running builder, the statuses `building`, `fixing` and `checking` (the owning session's check, [0022](0022-site-cards-build-in-parallel-when-their-files-dont-overlap.md)), a draft whose CI is running or red, and a PR ready for review whose CI is red ("Next: Fix the failing CI"), since fixing CI is Claude's job. A builder changing a PR that is in review moves it back to In Progress until it hands back.
- **In Review: done for now and waiting on a person.** The person is the owner, or the reviewers once a PR is ready for review. Stale stays as it is ([0044](0044-a-pr-in-review-shows-its-age-and-goes-stale-after-seven-days.md)).
- **The owner's waits** are, in order: an open question on the card (a pick between prototypes when the first open question links a `prototype-*.html` page, [0015](0015-prototypes-come-on-request.md)), a draft with green CI waiting for Ready ([0042](0042-the-board-is-the-sessions-work-log-and-review-stays-on-github.md)), an ask Claude put in progress for the owner's Done ([0035](0035-an-ask-can-wait-in-progress-until-the-owner-marks-it-done.md)), and manual steps on an approved PR, which monitor never merges itself. Each becomes the card's `action`, `{kind, url, label}`, and the label is its next step.
- **A card only the owner can move on goes to In Review**: a Todo card, such as one being grilled, and an In Progress card whose only wait is the owner's, such as a green draft or an ask in progress. Once the owner acts, the card returns to its own lane. A card Claude still works on keeps In Progress with its action, as does a card already waiting on the reviewers. The owner's message in an in-progress ask's thread puts the next step back with Claude until it is answered.
- **An Action needed badge** in the notice tone leads the chips of every card with an action, whatever its lane, and links to the thing to act on: the question on `questions.html`, where a prototype pick's takes show inline ([0048](0048-a-questions-takes-show-inline.md)), the PR's Ready on GitHub, or the card's thread for an ask's Done or manual steps (`actionChip` in `board/public/ui-cards.js`). It replaces the "open items" chip, and the open card's action row shows "Action needed" in Grill's place while questions are open. Reviewer waits get no badge; their chips show them.
- **A held card waits on nobody** ([0023](0023-a-card-can-be-held-and-every-card-button-confirms.md)): it keeps its lane and gets no badge, as it has no Needs you line.

An answered prototype ask sat in In Progress while it waited on the owner's pick, and a card waiting on prototypes was the only owner wait moved to In Review, by a special case in `buildBoard`. The owner couldn't tell from the lanes what waited on them.

The owner approved this on the `hub-orchestrator` ask that became INT-105 ("go for it"), adding that a card not done and not actively building while in progress should carry the badge, and that prototypes go to In Review with it and back to In Progress once building.
