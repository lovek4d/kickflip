# A sidebar of every board replaces the hub home

The hub home page is gone. Every board page carries a sidebar listing every site, so the owner switches boards without going back to the hub, and the hub's own controls sit at the sidebar's foot and on a status page at `/_hub/`.

- **The sidebar is the site list.** Boards first, then other sites under Pages, each row with its line icon and colour ([0017](0017-site-icons-are-line-icons-from-a-built-in-set.md)), its status word and lane chips ([0037](0037-the-hub-home-shows-status-not-descriptions.md)) and, on every site but the one showing, its waiting count. The current board's row is highlighted. The data comes from one hub endpoint, `/_hub/sidebar.json`, which the hub builds from the manifest, each site's `GET /_claude/needs` ([0010](0010-sites-report-what-waits-through-claude-needs.md)) and the board registry ([0040](0040-one-board-registry-for-every-hub-feature.md)); it pre-renders each row's icon and status line with `hub/home.mjs`, so the words have one source. `board/public/ui-sidebar.js` draws it, `sidebar.js` keeps it fresh every 30 seconds and paints this device's last copy at once, and `sidebar.css` styles it. Hub pages draw it on the hub with the same renderer.
- **`/` opens this device's last board.** Each board page stores its route in `localStorage` (`hub:lastBoard`, wrapped in try/catch) as it opens. `/`, which the Home Screen app starts at, is a small page that opens that board; a device with none, or one whose board has left the manifest, opens `/_hub/sidebar.json`'s `start`: the board with the most waiting, the most recently changed among equals. With no site at all it opens `/_hub/`. Board pages link the hub's web app manifest, so adding any board to the Home Screen starts the app at `/`.
- **On a phone the logo opens the sidebar as a drawer.** The kickflip in the board header opens it, and so does a swipe in from the left edge; a red count on the logo sums what waits on the other sites. The drawer's head repeats the logo left of "Boards" and closes with ✕ at its top right, the scrim, Escape or a swipe back. No header button was added. Without the sidebar script the logo links to `/_hub/`.
- **From 900px the sidebar is always shown** as a rail on the left. The logo moves into it, left of the Boards header, and the board header is just the title and its controls.
- **The foot holds the hub's rows**: Hub (its health word and how many sites are up), Notifications (on, off, held or snoozed, or off on this device), Polling (the current interval and the next check) and Settings (rules for every board). Each opens its hub page. While this device has no push subscription, a Turn on row leads the sidebar and opens hub Settings, which subscribes from a tap as iOS requires. The drawer scrolls when the rows don't fit.
- **`/_hub/` is the hub's status page.** It lists each site's health with each board's links and watcher ([0046](0046-hub-links-turn-red-only-after-a-grace-period.md)), whether the hub runs its own code, polling with the queue's Send now, this device's notifications and the rules for every board. The hub reads broken while a site is down or a board link reads broken; a link still settling doesn't count. Hub Orchestrator is a board like the others: it holds the work of improving the hub.

The owner picked take C, "Sidebar replaces the hub", on INT-99's first prototype (`sb-shape`), then on the second (`prototype-sidebar-2.html`): `sb2-root` last, `sb2-open` logo, `sb2-foot` rows and `sb2-backend` console. On INT-104 they placed the logo: left of the Boards header on desktop with the board header just the title, and left of whichever shows on a phone; and an overlay closes with an X at its top right. They added that the hub should end up as only a status page.

Considered:
- A drawer beside a hub home that stays, or a switcher under the board's title (`sb-shape` A, B).
- `/` opening the board that needs the owner most on every launch, or a Needs you page across boards (`sb2-root` urgent, needs). The first jumps between boards; the second is the hub home again.
- A ☰ button, or the logo opening a full-page list (`sb2-open` menu, page). One more control in a full header; two page loads per switch.
- One status line at the foot, or none with the controls on Hub Orchestrator (`sb2-foot` line, hub).
- Hub and Work tabs on Hub Orchestrator, or a health strip above its lanes (`sb2-backend` tabs, strip). Either makes one board's page different from the rest.
