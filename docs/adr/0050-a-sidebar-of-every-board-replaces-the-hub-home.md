# A sidebar of every board replaces the hub home

Superseded in part by [0055](0055-one-app-for-the-whole-hub.md): `/` serves the app shell, which picks the board by the same rule and stores the last board as each one opens; the shell mounts the sidebar once and keeps it fresh from the hub's one stream, not a 30-second poll.

Superseded in part by [0069](0069-the-hub-starts-a-new-boards-session.md) on New board's start: the hub starts the board's session itself once the request passes its checks, with no ask to a hub board, and a failed start reads Didn't start at once with the clone or session error line.

Superseded in part by [0070](0070-phone-headers-sit-at-the-bottom.md) on sidebar order, header position and title, and the 900px rail breakpoint.

The hub home page is gone. Every board page carries a sidebar listing every site, so the owner switches boards without going back to the hub, and the hub's own controls sit at the sidebar's top and on a status page at `/_hub/`.

- **The sidebar is the site list.** Hub and Settings first, then notifications, Pages and Boards, and Boards and Pages stay apart everywhere sites are listed: the sidebar, the hub's page and `/`'s pick, which takes only a board. Each row has its line icon and colour ([0017](0017-site-icons-are-line-icons-from-a-built-in-set.md)), its lane chips, wrapping, then its status word on the line below ([0037](0037-the-hub-home-shows-status-not-descriptions.md)). A row has no count of its own, since its "N need you" says the same. The current board's row is highlighted. The data comes from one hub endpoint, `/_hub/sidebar.json`, which the hub builds from the manifest, each site's `GET /_claude/needs` ([0010](0010-sites-report-what-waits-through-claude-needs.md)) and the board registry ([0040](0040-one-board-registry-for-every-hub-feature.md)); it pre-renders each row's icon and status line with `hub/home.mjs`, so the words have one source. `board/public/ui-sidebar.js` draws it, `sidebar.js` paints this device's last copy at once and each copy the hub's stream sends ([0055](0055-one-app-for-the-whole-hub.md)), and `sidebar.css` styles it.
- **New board ends the Boards group.** Its + tile opens the board’s usual sheet, above the bottom bar on a phone and centered from 900px, with a searchable GitHub repo picker first, then branch, Name and optional What it’s for ([0073](0073-a-new-board-chooses-a-repo-and-branch.md)). The repo's default branch and name are prefilled, and the sheet shows where the clone goes and which boards share it. Start stays disabled until a repo, branch and name are set; Cancel closes. `POST /_hub/new-board` validates the request, and the hub starts the board's session itself ([0069](0069-the-hub-starts-a-new-boards-session.md)). The manifest’s `starting` records keep the title and request time before a session exists and through its start; the sidebar shows them as muted, non-link Cloning… rows followed by Starting… until their routes join. A start that fails, or 15 minutes without a join, makes a row read Didn’t start with Dismiss, which removes the pending record. These rows and New board take no switch-mode keys.
- **`/` opens this device's last board.** Each board page stores its route in `localStorage` (`hub:lastBoard`, wrapped in try/catch) as it opens. `/`, which the Home Screen app starts at, is a small page that opens that board; a device with none, or one whose board has left the manifest, opens `/_hub/sidebar.json`'s `start`: the board with the most waiting, the most recently changed among equals. With no board at all it opens `/_hub/`. Board pages link the hub's web app manifest, so adding any board to the Home Screen starts the app at `/`.
- **The header's logo toggles the sidebar at every width.** It shows no count, since a badge hanging over its edge clipped on a phone; its label says what waits on the other sites. No header button was added. Without the sidebar script the logo links to `/_hub/`.
- **Below 900px the sidebar is a drawer.** The kickflip in the header opens it, and so does a swipe in from the left edge. The drawer's header sits at its foot, repeats the logo left of "Kickflip" and closes with that logo, ✕ at its right, the scrim, Escape or a swipe back. A window that narrows below 900px turns the rail into the drawer by itself, so a narrow window or an iPad in split view works like a phone.
- **From 900px the sidebar is a rail** on the left. The logo moves into it, left of the Kickflip header, and the board header is just the title and its controls. The rail's logo slides it out of sight, and the header's logo, back in its place, brings it back; this device remembers the choice (`hub:sidebarCollapsed`), and the page opens that way.
- **The utility group at the top holds Hub and Settings**: Hub shows its health word and how many sites are up; Settings opens Hub Settings, with Notifications controls and the Polling summary row first, then Codex and Rules. Settings' sub line shows when notifications are off, held or snoozed, or off or blocked on this device. The Settings row stays selected on Hub Settings and its child pages, including Polling, however they were opened, including from the hub page header's gear. While this device has no push subscription, a Turn on row follows Settings and opens hub Settings, which subscribes from a tap as iOS requires. The drawer's list scrolls when the rows don't fit, while its header stays visible.
- **`/_hub/` is the hub's status page.** It lists each site's health with each board's links and watcher ([0046](0046-hub-links-turn-red-only-after-a-grace-period.md)), whether the hub runs its own code, polling with the queue's Send now, this device's notifications and the rules for every board. The hub reads broken while a site is down or a board link reads broken; a link still settling doesn't count. Hub Orchestrator is a board like the others: it holds the work of improving the hub.

The owner picked take C, "Sidebar replaces the hub", on INT-99's first prototype (`sb-shape`), then on the second (`prototype-sidebar-2.html`): `sb2-root` last, `sb2-open` logo, `sb2-foot` rows and `sb2-backend` console. On INT-104 they placed the logo: left of the Boards header on desktop with the board header just the title, and left of whichever shows on a phone; and an overlay closes with an X at its top right. They added that the hub should end up as only a status page. On INT-117 they asked that the logo collapse the sidebar, that it collapse by itself when the view is too narrow, and that Boards and Pages stay apart everywhere; from its prototype (`spa-collapse`) they picked a collapsed rail that is hidden, with the drawer below 1000px. On INT-122 they dropped the logo's count after it clipped on a phone.

Considered:
- A drawer beside a hub home that stays, or a switcher under the board's title (`sb-shape` A, B).
- `/` opening the board that needs the owner most on every launch, or a Needs you page across boards (`sb2-root` urgent, needs). The first jumps between boards; the second is the hub home again.
- A ☰ button, or the logo opening a full-page list (`sb2-open` menu, page). One more control in a full header; two page loads per switch.
- One status line at the foot, or none with the controls on Hub Orchestrator (`sb2-foot` line, hub).
- A collapsed rail as a 60px strip of site icons, each with its own count, which stays down to phone width in place of the drawer (`spa-collapse` strip). It costs a sixth of a phone's width and tells boards apart by icon alone.
- Hub and Work tabs on Hub Orchestrator, or a health strip above its lanes (`sb2-backend` tabs, strip). Either makes one board's page different from the rest.
