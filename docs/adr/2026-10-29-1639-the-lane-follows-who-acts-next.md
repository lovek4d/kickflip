# The lane follows who acts next, and a card waiting on the owner says Action needed

Superseded in part by [an unstarted card waits on its questions in Todo, and its one action button follows what it waits on](2026-10-30-1309-an-unstarted-card-waits-on-its-questions-in-todo.md) on the owner's questions: a card that hasn't started and waits only on its open questions or a prototype pick stays in Todo with Action needed, and a started one keeps its lane. In Review keeps the owner's other waits below. The open card's action row shows Open questions, not "Action needed", while questions are open.

A card's lane answers one question: who acts next? `board/domain.mjs` decides it in two steps. `laneOf` places the card by what Claude and GitHub say, and `placeOwnerWait` moves a card only the owner can move on to In Review and gives it its action.

- **In Progress: Claude or a builder is working.** That covers a running builder, the status `checking` (the owning session's check, [site cards build in parallel when their files don't overlap](2026-10-29-1639-site-cards-build-in-parallel-when-their-files-dont-overlap.md)), a draft whose CI is running or red, and a PR ready for review whose CI is red ("Next: Fix the failing CI"), since fixing CI is Claude's job. `building` and `fixing` count only while a builder record on that card is open; with none open, the PR and other state place it as if the status were absent ([a card derives its lane and its review next step from GitHub, never from a kept status](2026-10-29-1639-a-card-derives-its-lane-from-github.md)). A builder changing a PR that is in review moves it back to In Progress until it hands back.
- **In Review: done for now and waiting on a person.** The person is the owner, or the reviewers once a PR is ready for review. Stale stays as it is ([a PR in review shows its age, and goes Stale after seven days](2026-10-29-1639-a-pr-in-review-shows-its-age-and-goes-stale-after-seven-days.md)).
- **The owner's waits** are, in order: an open question on the card (a pick between prototypes when the first open question links a `prototype-*.html` page, [prototypes come only when obviously helpful or on request](2026-10-29-1639-prototypes-come-on-request.md)), a draft with green CI waiting for Ready ([the board is the session's work log, and review stays on GitHub](2026-10-29-1639-the-board-is-the-sessions-work-log-and-review-stays-on-github.md)), an ask Claude put in progress for the owner's Done ([an ask can wait on the owner before Done, and the owner closes any open card from its thread](2026-10-29-1639-an-ask-can-wait-in-progress-until-the-owner-marks-it-done.md)), manual steps on an approved PR, which monitor never merges itself, and, while the board's auto-merge is off, the Merge of an approved PR with green CI and a CLEAN merge state ([Auto-merge is a board setting, off by default, and the owner merges from the card](2026-10-29-1752-auto-merge-is-a-board-setting-off-by-default.md)). Each becomes the card's `action`, `{kind, url, label}`, and the label is its next step.
- **A card only the owner can move on goes to In Review**: a Todo card, such as one being grilled, and an In Progress card whose only wait is the owner's, such as a green draft or an ask in progress. Once the owner acts, the card returns to its own lane. A card Claude still works on keeps In Progress with its action, as does a card already waiting on the reviewers. The owner's message in an in-progress ask's thread puts the next step back with Claude until it is answered.
- **An Action needed badge** in the notice tone leads the chips of every card with an action, after only its unread chip, whatever its lane, and links to the thing to act on: the question on `questions.html`, where a prototype pick's takes show inline ([a question's takes show inline, and back goes one step](2026-10-29-1639-a-questions-takes-show-inline.md)), the PR's Ready on GitHub, or the card's thread for an ask's Done, manual steps or Merge (`actionChip` in `board/public/ui-cards.js`). It replaces the "open items" chip, and the open card's action row shows "Action needed" in Grill's place while questions are open. Reviewer waits get no badge; their chips show them.
- **A held card waits on nobody** ([a card can be held, and every card button confirms](2026-10-29-1639-a-card-can-be-held-and-every-card-button-confirms.md)): it keeps its lane and gets no badge, as it has no Needs you line.

An answered prototype ask sat in In Progress while it waited on the owner's pick, and a card waiting on prototypes was the only owner wait moved to In Review, by a special case in `buildBoard`. The owner couldn't tell from the lanes what waited on them.

The owner approved this on the `hub-orchestrator` ask that became INT-105 ("go for it"), adding that a card not done and not actively building while in progress should carry the badge, and that prototypes go to In Review with it and back to In Progress once building.
