# Waves are bands in Todo, each with its next card's Implement

The owner, on #156, settled on take C of `prototypes/wave-groups-3`: "Waves show as lane-style bands inside Todo … no goal line; the goal shows on hover (title) or long-press … only the wave with a ready card starts expanded; cards in wave order with step numbers; done cards fold into "N done"; a ⋯ menu moves a card up or down … Each wave's band carries Implement (starts its next ready card) or "next: #N <state>" … Loose Todo cards below the bands." This replaces the Waves section above the lanes and its tap-to-filter ([0109](0109-a-wave-the-owner-asks-for-is-a-lineup-of-cards.md) keeps the lineup itself).

- **Bands come from the board.** `domain.mjs` `waveBandsOf` gives each open lineup, then on a board over ~/.claude/waves each unfinished wave file, as `{kind, slug, title, goal, keys, next, merged, total, triage}`. A lineup's keys are in build order and its `next` is its first card still in Todo, `ready` when that card is ready and buildable, else a word for why not: held, "waits on #N", needs you, new, its state, or not ready. A wave file's band has its cards in lane order and no `next`, step numbers or ⋯, since it has no order.
- **A band holds only Todo cards.** Its started cards stay in their lanes and read in its head ("#60 in progress"); its Done cards are one "N done · #a, #b" line whose refs open the card in Done. A card shows in one place, so its open state and thread stay one element.
- **Open state.** Until the owner taps a band's head, only the first band whose next card is ready (a wave file's: any ready card) is open; a tap is kept per band in storage. A #item= link or a Needs you line opens its card's band.
- **Implement is the card's own tap.** The band's Implement is the open card's Implement button for the next card (`card-build`, the same confirm, reading Starting… while sent).
- **A move waits for the server.** ⋯ Move up / Move down send `lineup-move {lineup, key, by}`. The server reorders the lineup's Todo cards among the places they hold (`domain.mjs` `lineupMoved`), so a started or done card never moves and nothing started becomes blocked, writes it through `store.mjs` `changeLineup`'s `order`, which chains the cards again, and answers with the board. It appends no event: the order is the board's to keep, and nothing in it needs the session. Sessions reorder with `lineup set <slug> --order`.
- **The goal is not a line.** It is the band name's `title`, and a long-press on touch opens it as a popover; the click that ends the press doesn't fold the band.
