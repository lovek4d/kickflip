# A status line too long is refused at write, through one length rule

The owner (T-138, filed as T-247) saw two status lines lose their point at the ellipsis. "Marked as ready · <check>" cut the orchestrator's `--text` at 100 characters. "Couldn't start implementing" quoted T-138's whole title first, so `fit` cut the line before it reached the reason. The owner asked for one shared helper that every status line goes through, and for text that runs too long to be rewritten to fit, not cut.

- **One rule, `lineFault(words, room)`** in `board/hub-lines.mjs`, beside `fit`. It returns why the words run past their room ("runs to N characters, past R"), or null. The room is `BRIEF_LINE_LIMIT`, less whatever a line's fixed lead takes. `hubText` checks every kind's words with it, and the store checks `builder progress` details (room 80, which leaves 20 for the "Codex checking: " head), question text and the readied check.
- **Text an author writes is refused, never cut.** From staging, `item set --phase ready --text` is the line "Marked as ready · <text>". It must be one line of at most 82 characters, and is refused before anything changes, naming the length and the room. `--from-ask`'s `--text` is a message of its own and no longer appears in the readied line.
- **Lines the board writes are worded to fit.** A kind whose inputs are all bounded reads whole: hub-lines.test.mjs runs every kind at its longest inputs and asserts no ellipsis. Only an unbounded input, such as an error or a path, is shortened by `fit`, and a fault line's detail keeps it whole.
- **A refusal can carry its own short reason.** `BoardError` takes a `brief`, its reason sized for a status line, and `build-refused` reads that brief when it has one. A title refusal's brief, from `startTitleBrief`, is "its title isn't <type>(<scope>): <summary>; retitle it". It quotes no title, and the line's detail keeps the whole refusal with the title and the `item set --title` command.
- **Readying checks the title.** `item set --phase ready` refuses a title that `builder start` would refuse (`startTitleFault`). A `--title` in the same call fixes it, so a bad title is caught when the card is readied, not when the pull fails.
