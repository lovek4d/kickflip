# One click on a pill focuses its card, and the topic's title opens it

Card T-64. The owner: "any click on a topic pill on desktop should focus the relevant ticket on desktop", then "aim to simplify, throw out the double click handling".

On desktop (the embedded pane) one click on a pill selects its topic and focuses its card on the board. On a phone a tap only selects the topic. The two-tap rule and its `lastTap` state are gone. The topic's title above the questions is now a larger header button that opens the card (the pane focuses it; a phone opens the card's page). This replaces the two-tap rule of [open-items pills read the whole title](2026-11-07-2002-open-items-pills-read-the-whole-title-and-two-taps-open-the-card.md) and the double-click of [#316](2026-11-06-0100-double-clicking-a-pill-focuses-its-card-on-the-board.md).
