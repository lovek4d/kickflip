# A Todo card that nothing blocks has Build

The owner asked on hub/INT-261: "todo items should have a build button, idk why it went away. make it rightmost in the row". Cards still start on their own once their questions are answered and nothing blocks them ([notes replace the full plan, and a card starts on its own](2026-10-29-1816-notes-replace-the-full-plan-and-cards-start-on-their-own.md)), but some sit in Todo with nothing left to settle, such as one waiting for a prototype or a grill nobody needs. Build is the owner's "start it now". In the prototype round (todo-build A) the owner settled that Build shows only on cards nothing blocks: "open questions should also prevent build (rather action needed should block build)". This supersedes in part [notes replace the full plan, and a card starts on its own](2026-10-29-1816-notes-replace-the-full-plan-and-cards-start-on-their-own.md)'s "no go button", and [a board's build script serves or builds each card and main](2026-11-01-1846-a-boards-build-script-serves-or-builds-each-card-and-main.md)'s Serve or Build as the rightmost workflow button.

- **Which cards.** `board/domain.mjs` marks a card `buildable` when it is a work card still unstarted in Todo ([a started card never returns to Todo](2026-10-29-1639-a-started-card-never-returns-to-todo.md)), not held, with no unfinished dep ([cards wait on explicit blocked-by links](2026-10-29-1639-cards-wait-on-explicit-blocked-by-links.md)) and no Action needed, so no open question or prototype pick. A held card waits for Resume instead. A follow-up keeps its own Build ([follow-ups are Todo cards of their wave](2026-10-29-1639-follow-ups-are-todo-cards-of-their-wave.md)).
- **Where.** The open card's workflow row ends with Build, after the action button and Serve or Build, filled orange like a follow-up's Build. Build is not Action needed: nothing waits on the owner, so it adds no badge, push or Needs you line.
- **What a tap does.** It confirms "Build <ref>? Claude starts its builder now with the notes as they stand.", then sends `card-build {key}`. The button reads "Starting…", disabled, while the event is sent and until the card's status changes or Claude writes a thread line other than a status line after the tap. A started builder moves the card to In Progress, where it has no Build.
- **What the session does.** On `card-build` it starts the card's builder now, with its notes as they stand. When it can't, because the card was held, blocked or got a question since the tap, it says why in one thread line, which ends "Starting…".

Considered:
- Build on every Todo card, overriding its blockers (todo-build B): the owner chose to keep blocked cards waiting on their deps.
- Build on a card with open questions, closing them with their recommended options: the owner chose that Action needed blocks Build, so the questions are answered first.
- The `--await-build` flag of hub/INT-121, where the session asked for the go: dropped in [notes replace the full plan, and a card starts on its own](2026-10-29-1816-notes-replace-the-full-plan-and-cards-start-on-their-own.md), since every tap confirmed what the answers had settled. This Build is the owner's choice, not a step the session waits on.
