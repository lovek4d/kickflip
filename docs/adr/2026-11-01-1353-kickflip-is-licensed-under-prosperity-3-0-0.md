# Kickflip is licensed under Prosperity 3.0.0

The owner picked option B of `license` on hub/INT-197: kickflip is free for people and hobbyists, but companies don't get it for free, and Ever does.

- `LICENSE` is the Prosperity Public License 3.0.0 verbatim, with its Contributor and Source Code lines filled in. It allows noncommercial use for free and a 30-day trial for commercial use.
- Any other company use needs a commercial license from Love Kharod. Ever holds one for free with no end date, stated in `README.md`.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` has contributors license their work to Love Kharod with the right to relicense it, so he can still grant commercial licenses that cover it.
- AGPL-3.0 was ruled out: kickflip runs on each person's own Mac, so a company could use it internally without sharing anything.
