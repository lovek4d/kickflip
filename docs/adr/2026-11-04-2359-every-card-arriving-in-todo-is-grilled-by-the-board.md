# Every card arriving in Todo is grilled by the board

Before this, a card got a planner only when the orchestrator remembered to dispatch one, or when the idle pull's plan picked it: one card at a time for the whole board, only while nothing was building or in review and nothing was pullable. On a busy board, follow-ups filed with `item add` (#202, #205, #209) sat in Todo ungrilled. The owner (#216): "when a real item lands in to do … it should immediately go into prototype or grill, unless it is a quick build", and "the orchestrator should not have to watch the to-do lane". On the grill's questions (gh216-pick): "maybe we should ALWAYS grill first, and inform the grill to suggest prototypes if relevant".

- **Arrival is read from the built board.** `board/domain.mjs` `arrivalsOf` lists the open work cards in Todo that nothing has picked up: buildable, not ready, held, queued by a Build tap or worked on by a session, with no planner record ever, no grill or prototype asked for and no question topic. So an ask filed `--state grilling`, `item add`, the owner's +, an import and any route added later all count the same, without depending on which command made the card. A card with a question topic has had its questions already, so a card grilled by hand before planners were recorded isn't grilled again. A reopened card lands in In Progress, so it never arrives.
- **The board launches the grill.** With Auto implement on, the board server's Auto pass (`server.mjs` `launchArrivals`) opens a planner record on each arrival, a prototype's for a card filed `--state prototyping` and a grill's for any other, and launches it headless through the planner launch the hub already has ([the hub launches every agent from the card](2026-11-04-2130-the-hub-launches-every-agent-from-the-card.md)), the planner's own status line showing the grill (or prototype) running, with no arrival line of the board's own Its open record keeps the card off the list, and a finished one keeps it off for good: one grill per arrival. No event wakes the session.
- **A grill unless the owner asked for a prototype.** The owner's "always grill first" (gh216-pick) covers an arrival with no stated intent: filed `--state grilling` or with no state, it gets a grill. A card filed `--state prototyping` means the owner asked for one ("prototype this: …"), so it gets a prototype planner, as a prototype request does. The grill's brief (`headless.mjs` `plannerBrief`) asks it to end with a question offering a prototype round when the card changes the hub's or a board's UI, so the owner picks the round or taps its eye. That keeps the rule that UI cards are prototyped before a builder.
- **Its own cap.** A board setting, Maximum parallel planners (`max_planners`, 1 to 99, 3 by default), sits under Maximum parallel implementers in Board settings' Auto section and in `settings set max-planners`. Running grills and prototypes count against it; builds don't, and planners still don't count against the implementers ([planners and implementers are two kinds of agent](2026-11-03-1551-planners-and-implementers-are-two-kinds-of-agent.md)). Arrivals past the cap wait in board order. The usage pause holds launches as it holds builds; the minute check launches them once it lifts.
- **Quick builds skip it.** Ready is the skip. `item add` takes `--state ready` (only ready), with the size and notes file the Ready check asks for, checked before the card's ticket is made, so a quick follow-up never passes through Todo unready.
- **The idle plan is retired.** `card-plan`, its `data/planned.json` marker and pullOf's `plannable` go: arrivals replace them. Its fallback for a card prototyping again after a pick of no take goes too: that card had its planner, and the owner-answer's `prototype_again` wake to the session covers it.
- **The orchestrator files and reacts.** When filing it writes the card's notes, the grill's brief, and picks ready or nothing. It no longer watches Todo, dispatches grills or writes their prompts. A grill's questions go straight to the owner (gh216-after), and the orchestrator acts on the owner-answer as before.

Considered:
- launching on arrival with Auto implement off too: the owner asked for no watching, but a board with its Auto switch off has said it starts no agent by itself, and every grill spends usage. The switch's line now says it grills new Todo cards.
- a prototype straight away for UI cards, picked by the filer: the owner chose grill first, with the grill offering the round.
- the orchestrator reviewing a grill's findings before the owner sees them: a wake and a delay on every card; the owner chose direct.
- tracking launched arrivals in a marker file, as the pull does: the grill record already says the card had its planner, across restarts.
