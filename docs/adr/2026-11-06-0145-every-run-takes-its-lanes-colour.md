# Every run takes its lane's colour, and one page reviews every bar

The owner, on #237's look (11/05): "colors again need to align with lane", and a structured review of which progress bar shows in which lane, when, under what name. #324's answers: by lane, "Todo can actually just be gray" (gh324-colour); a rebase run stays In Review as a status line in that lane's colour, no bar (gh324-rebase-lane); an editable review page (gh324-page), shaped as the board itself, tap a bar to edit it beneath (gh324-proto-look).

- **A run's line, dot and bar take its card's lane colour.** ui-cards.js `tone()` gives every status line on a Todo, In Progress or In Review card the lane class `in-todo`, `in-progress` or `in-review`: grey (`--muted`), orange (`--color-notice`), blue (`--color-info`). A Todo planner's dashes take tone `in-todo`, grey. A Done card's line keeps the plain orange. The quiet, waiting and stalled lines keep their own colours over the lane's. This supersedes the Todo blue of [each lane is a queue for the orchestrator](2026-11-04-2300-each-lane-is-a-queue-for-the-orchestrator.md) and the In Review fallback to orange.
- **One pulse for every bar.** `@keyframes pulse-fill` dims the bar's own `--bar-fill`, so a bar pulses in its lane's colour; the planner's separate `pulse-fill-plan` is gone.
- **The thread keeps its colours.** A run's fold and lines in the thread stay by agent: a builder's orange, a planner's owner blue, a finished run's green ([a finished run's lines turn green](2026-11-07-1200-a-finished-runs-lines-turn-green.md)). The sidebar's board start steps stay orange: they belong to no card.
- **The review page is `progress-review.html`.** A board page, shown in the shell's frame at `/<route>/progress-review.html`. It draws the board's three lanes with one made-up card per bar or status line: grilling and prototyping in Todo; build, orchestrator review, install and board start in In Progress; rebase run, merging and land stopped in In Review. Each card is drawn by the real renderer (`workLines`, the sidebar's `startSteps`), its steps read from `BUILD_PHASES` and `START_STEPS`. A tapped card opens its editor beneath: name, lane, and its steps' names and order (drag, or the arrows). Each lane has a colour pick, which recolours its column. Send posts the whole review as one owner `ask`, the event the + makes, each change marked with what the board draws now; the orchestrator files it as its own card. A reply on a card's thread was ruled out: the page reviews the whole board, and a closed card's thread goes unseen.
