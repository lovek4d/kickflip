# New asks sit in a New strip above the lanes

#586 (hub/T-171). The owner: a sent task's message should stay out of the card until the orchestrator has titled it, with no duplicate between header and thread, and editing should happen where the message shows. The discussion settled that an ask is not a card to the owner until the orchestrator ingests it; the prototype round (take A) put the strip at the top of the board, and the owner titled it "New" and its working word "processing...".

Amends [every ask is a task card from the moment it arrives](2026-11-09-1900-every-ask-is-a-task-card-from-the-moment-it-arrives.md) (its **New** section: where a New card draws, its bump, and the merge banner). Supersedes [a New card shows its ticket chip from the moment it lands](2026-11-11-2133-a-new-card-shows-its-ticket-chip-from-the-moment-it-lands.md), since no New card draws, and the landing flash of [a new task lands at the bottom of Todo and flashes blue](2026-11-06-0300-a-new-task-lands-at-the-bottom-of-todo-and-flashes-blue.md); the flash as the card is ingested into Todo stays.

- **The data stays.** The board server still files a `T-<n>` card the moment an ask arrives, and it is `isNew` until sorted. The page draws no lane card for it: `lanesHtml` leaves `isNew` cards out, and they draw in the New strip instead.
- **The strip.** `#newStrip`, above the lanes on every width and below main's bar, headed like a lane: "New" and its count. It hides when it has no entry. Entries are oldest sent first (`ui-cards.js` `newEntries`).
- **Waiting and processing.** An ask still New shows the owner's whole message, then "Moved from …" and "Edited" when they apply, and a grey "waiting…" until the watcher picks it up, then an orange "processing..." with the working dot (a paused board reads paused). It carries Edit and Delete, no bump. Edit opens the edit box in the entry itself (`state.edits`, drawn by `newEntry`), and Save sends `ask-edited` as a thread edit does. Delete confirms and sends `ask-withdrawn`, as before.
- **Ingested.** The first phase a card from an ask gets stamps `sorted_at` on the item (`store.mjs` `leaveNew`, from `item set --phase`, a reply that starts its discussion, a stage, or a brief), and the board sends it as `ingested {at, text}` with the owner's message. While the card's read mark is older than `at`, the strip shows the message and "Ticket created: T-177 · <title>", a blue link to the card. An ask merged into another card (`item relate-ask`) shows "Added to T-123" from the board's `merges`, which now carry the ask's id, sent time and whole message. Each has Dismiss, with no confirm.
- **Dismiss is a read mark.** Dismiss marks the card read at `at` (`/api/read`), on every device, so it needs no record of its own. Following the entry's link marks the same and opens the card; opening the card any other way reads it only up to its newest message, which clears the entry once that message is the ingest's or later. A merge left the answer banner, which shows answers alone.
- **Older cards.** A card sorted before `sorted_at` existed has no stamp, so the strip never fills with past asks.

Considered:
- A dismiss record per ask, kept apart from read marks: one more store and endpoint, for the same "has the owner seen it" fact the read mark already holds.
- Deriving the ingest time from the first Claude line in the card's thread: a card filed grilling writes no line until its planner runs, so the time would drift.
- Keeping the bump on a New entry: the strip lists asks oldest first, and order there means nothing until the card is in Todo.
