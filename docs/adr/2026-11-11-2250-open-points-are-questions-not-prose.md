# Open points are questions, not prose

The orchestrator posts the open points and choices of a discussion reply as questions with `question add`, as a grill would, instead of listing them in the reply. The reply says how many questions wait on the card.

- `question add` needs no grill: it works on a card the orchestrator is discussing, and leaves it discussing. It moves only a ready or staging card to grilling.
- The habit lives in the product, not in the orchestrator's memory: SKILL.md says it, and `reply`'s output and the watcher's line for a thread message name `question add` as the way to ask.
