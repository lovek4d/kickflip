# A card owing nobody, or with the owner's word last, waits on the orchestrator

T-273 sat in discussing for two days with no Implement mark, no open question and no planner. Nothing on the board waited on anyone for it: the owner had no Implement to tap, the orchestrator had no wait, and no sweep looked at it. On 11/15, 49 Todo cards were in that state. And an owner message on a Todo or In Progress card made no wait at all: only a reopened card idle in In Review had a `reply` wait, from its oldest unanswered message.

Two orchestrator waits now cover both (domain.mjs `orchestratorWaitsOf`):

- **reply**, the owner's word last. A card in any lane but Done whose newest thread message is the owner's waits on the orchestrator, whatever its marks or other waits, from the first owner message since the orchestrator last spoke and since the card last closed. Any orchestrator line after it ends the wait (`answersOwner`): a message, a reply to the event, and status lines (moves, staged, readied, pulled and the board's other hub lines), since an action that answers the owner always leaves one. A builder's progress line, agent line or ask doesn't. This replaces the reopened card's reply wait rather than sitting beside it; the In Review lane placement of a reopened card (`ownerPending`, "Your reply waits on the orchestrator") still reads its messages' answered state. Messages older than a restart count, since the rule reads the thread, not delivered events. The card's own ask is left to the intake wait, and an owner's answer to the `answer` wait.
- **limbo**, owes nobody anything. A sorted Todo card in new or discussing with no owner action (no live Implement or Close mark, no open question, no script), no brief owed or waiting to launch, and no planner running or queued waits at once (the owner, 11/15: no 30-minute guard), next "T-n owes nobody anything: ask Implement, ask questions, or close it". It ends when the card gets a mark, a question, a brief, a planner, a phase change or Done. Its since is when the card last changed state (its creation or sorting, a lapsed mark's time, a planner's end, a board or command line, an owner's answer), so chat on the card doesn't re-add it.

The limbo waits come last on the waits list, oldest first (`orchestratorWaits`), so the cards already in limbo surface once at deploy as one batch behind the real work. The header's waits chip never reads them red, which marks a wait past the old 30-minute push: they are the orchestrator's backlog, and 49 red rows at deploy would bury the rest.

Each reply wait that ends appends `{key, ref, messageAt, endedAt, endedBy, minutes}` to `data/limbo-waits.jsonl` (domain.mjs `ownerWordEnds`, server.mjs `stepWaits`), `endedBy` the line that ended it or null when the card closed or left the board, so an audit (T-440) can find messages "answered" by a status line alone.
