# A question and its options stand alone, its visual linked

A question's context and each option's label and detail must be answerable as they stand. T-604's question offered "L4 and L1", "All four" and "L4 only", labels defined only on a visual, so the owner couldn't answer without opening it.

- A question about something a visual or prototype shows carries that page as its `url`. The questions sheet draws a link row under the context, "See the visual ↗" for a visual, which opens in the reader sheet stacked on the questions sheet. The page gives detail, never the meaning. Option details stay open under each label. The sheet keeps #1024's size.
- `question add` refuses an option labelled only by codes (`/^[A-Z]\d+( and [A-Z]\d+)*$/`) with no detail, and a question with no `url` whose context names the card's visual (the word "visual" or a visual's file name) or recorded prototype (the word "prototype"). A refusal rather than a warning, as for the code-only labels.
- SKILL.md and the grill and prototype planner briefs state the rule.

The link row says "See the visual" rather than the page's title: a question carries only its url, and a visual's title lives in the thread line that placed it.
The visual and reviewer briefs write no questions, so they are left out of the rule.
