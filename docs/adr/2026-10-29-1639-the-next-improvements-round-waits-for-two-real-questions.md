# The next Improvements round waits for two real questions

The session writes the next Improvements round only once it holds at least two real questions about the site: choices the owner hasn't settled that came out of builds, asks, thread messages or friction seen while running the board. Until then the bulb rests, and a tap on it still asks for a round. A single question that blocks a card goes on that card instead, as a question in `owner.json`.

A round after each sent round ([the next Improvements round follows each sent round](2026-10-29-1639-the-next-improvements-round-follows-each-sent-round.md), which this replaces) produced rounds with one question, or questions written to fill the round.

Considered:
- right after each sent round, as before;
- once a day, with the morning push.

The owner chose two real questions (round `site-v22`, question `v22-cadence`, option A).
