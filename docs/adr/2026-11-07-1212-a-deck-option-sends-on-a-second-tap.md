# A deck option sends on a second tap

On a phone, a tap on a question's option in the Needs you deck only drafted the answer and moved to the next thing; Send on the questions page sent it. The owner asked that choosing an answer in Needs you actually send it, with a tap again to confirm (#430).

Now the first tap marks the option: it is drafted in the questions page's store (`qdraft:<id>`, keeping any drafted note), and the option turns into Send “<label>”, filled in the notice color. The deck stays on the question. A tap on another option moves the mark. A tap on the marked option sends that answer as `owner-answers`, with the question's drafted note and files, as the questions page's Send does, and spins until the board shows it answered, which takes it off the deck. A refusal toasts its reason and leaves the mark. A draft made on the questions page shows marked in the deck the same way, so one tap there sends it.

The prototype round (topic confirm-tap) drew three takes of the second tap; the owner picked the marked option turning into Send over a "Tap again to send" line under the marked option and a separate Send button under the options.

This supersedes the deck's "Draft, then Send" in [Needs you is a deck on a phone and a pane on a desktop](2026-11-05-0408-needs-you-is-a-deck-on-a-phone-and-a-pane-on-a-desktop.md). The owner's earlier worry there, that sending at once would hurt notes, holds less now: the first tap only marks, and a note drafted on the questions page goes with the send. The desktop pane is unchanged: its Waiting on you keeps radio picks and Send.
