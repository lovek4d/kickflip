# A wave header is its name, Notes and the counts

Todo's wave header reads: chevron, the wave's name, then at the right Notes and the x/y/k/z counts.

The header has no next section: no "next: #N …" text and no Implement button. A wave's card builds from its own card. The plain Todo count and the "· #N in progress" words are gone too, since the counts already say both.

`band.next` stays in the board data: Todo's first-open band is picked by it (`todoRows`).
