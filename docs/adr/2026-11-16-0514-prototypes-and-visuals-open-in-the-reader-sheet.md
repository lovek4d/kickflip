# Prototypes and visuals open in the reader sheet

The owner, on #857: "Visuals prototypes and grill should all show on a notes/questions modal. I'm thinking any ticket data that must open over should use that." Notes and logs already opened in the reader sheet (`app-reader.js`) and a card's questions, grill rounds included, in the questions sheet of the same shape. A prototype opened as the frame view on a phone, replacing the board, and from 900px in the board's right pane in place of Waiting on you (`app-pane.js` viewer). A visual opened as the frame view at every width. Either way the card left the screen. The rule now: any card data that opens over the board uses the sheet.

- **A card page.** A board's `prototype-<topic>.html` or `visuals/<card id>/<slug>.html` (`router.js isCardPage`, the paths `store.mjs` `PROTOTYPE_PAGE` and `VISUAL_PAGE` accept) is a card page. It is never a view of its own.
- **Every way in goes through the shell.** `shell.js go` takes a card page's URL at every width the way it takes a questions URL from 900px: it shows the board at the board's URL and asks the board to open the page (`B.openPage`), retrying until the board has mounted. That covers a thread's link line, a question's View prototypes, a Notes link, a push, a cold load and a link in a framed page (`view-frame.js` sends a card page's tap to the shell, and `framed` hands one the frame lands on). A Pick a take row opens the board's sheet directly in the desktop pane (`ctx.openPage`). On the phone's own Waiting on you page it goes through the shell to the board, since only the board has the sheet. Closing then leaves the owner on the board.
- **The sheet.** `openPage` frames the page in the reader sheet's body. The header carries the kind (prototype or visual), the card's ref and the page's title once it loads, the card's Round pill for a prototype (its rounds drop over the page and open in place), and Open alone ↗ (`BoardUI.aloneHref`, shared with the frame view). A take's ‹ › pager and Choose this stay the page's own footer (`prototype-switcher.js`), which hides its ✕ and Round pill in the sheet (`.ps-in-sheet`, renamed from `.ps-pane`).
- **Size.** From 900px the sheet widens for a page to 60% of the room right of the sidebar, or the pane's width when that is wider, the lanes still beside it. It lies over the questions sheet (z-index 31), so a prototype opened from a question's View prototypes covers it, and closing the prototype shows the question again. On a phone it is the modal sheet, near full height.
- **Open and close as the other sheets.** The same page opened again stays put. A round, or a card page a page links, opens in place. The open sheet keeps one history entry whatever it shows next (`show` pushes only when it has none), so Back and a close take it back once. Any other link in the page, and Choose this or a push sending a picked question on to its card's thread (`reader-leave` from `replaceWith`), close the sheet first and then open through the shell. The page's frame goes once the sheet has closed.

- **A markdown link.** A `[label](url)` in a note, a reply, an orchestrator message or a transcript's text opens in place when it names a page on this hub (a relative or same-origin URL, `ui-links.js opensHere`), as a bare URL already did, so the shell sends a card page to the sheet and a log or notes link to its own. It had always carried `target="_blank"`, which the shell and the sheet leave to the browser, so a visual linked that way opened in a new tab. Only another origin opens a new tab.

**Removed**, as it served only the prototype outside the sheet:
- `app-pane.js` viewer: `openViewer`, `paintViewer`, `closeViewer`, its click handler, its hashchange close, the `viewer` state, and `focusQuestion`'s `{sheet: false}`, which only ‹ Open questions used;
- the pane viewer's CSS (`.board-pane.viewing`, `.widened`, `.pane-viewer`, `.pv-top`, `.pv-frame`, the pane's `.ps-sheet`) and the col handle's widened rule;
- the prototype level of the page tree: `router.js` `isPrototype`, `questionOf` and the `wide` option of `levelOf`, `parentsOf` and `listen`. The tree is the board and its Waiting on you;
- `view-frame.js upTap` and the shell's `ctx.up`, which took a framed prototype's back arrow up the tree;
- the AppShell path in `view-questions.js openPrototype` for the embedded pane.
