# Old asks migrate into cards, and the ask-only records retire

#487 (hub/T-3). Since [every ask is a task card from the moment it arrives](2026-11-09-1900-every-ask-is-a-task-card-from-the-moment-it-arrives.md), a new ask is a card, but the asks handled before it still drew as their own rows, on their own records: an ask's progress mark, its triage title, the owner's ask-done and ask-bump events, and questions on an `item:ask:<id>` topic. The owner: those asks should become cards too, and the ask-only machinery should go.

It supersedes [a folded ask stays in Asks and says where it went](2026-11-05-0520-a-folded-ask-stays-in-asks-and-says-where-it-went.md), [an ask can wait on the owner before Done](2026-10-29-1639-an-ask-can-wait-in-progress-until-the-owner-marks-it-done.md) and [answered asks fold into the one Done list](2026-11-09-1430-answered-asks-fold-into-the-one-done-list.md), and the Ask badge and ask-row titling of [an ask is titled in place and wears an Ask badge](2026-11-10-1200-an-ask-is-titled-in-place-and-wears-an-ask-badge.md). It amends the 1900 ADR's "Older asks" and its waves-board exception.

- **The migration.** `store.mjs` `fileAsks`, under the tickets lock, makes every ask with no card a `T-<n>` card in the order it was asked. It skips an ask the owner deleted, one folded into a card (`ask-links.json`) or one that already became a card (`from_ask`), and one moved to another board (its thread's `ask-moved` line). The card's `created` is the ask's sent time.
- **Answered asks close in Done.** An ask Claude had answered goes to Done with `closed_at` at its answer time: the latest of its reply files and Claude's lines in its thread, or, for an ask closed only by a mark, the later of Claude's done mark and the owner's ask-done. Every progress mark on the boards was `done`, so none maps to In Review. The card then leaves through the server's Done archive pass like any closed card.
- **Titles.** A card takes the title triage gave its ask in `ask-titles.json`, else the ask's first line, as an arriving ask's card does.
- **Questions follow.** Each `item:ask:<id>` question in `owner.json` moves to its card's `item:<file>/<id>` topic, for an ask already carded or folded as well as one filed now. A question left on a deleted or moved ask's topic stays as it was.
- **When it runs.** The board server runs it at every start, and after a change when `buildBoard` lists an ask with no card (`unfiled`). It is idempotent: a second run files nothing. It deletes `ask-progress.json` and `ask-titles.json` once read.
- **No ask row.** `buildBoard` draws no item for an ask: `askItem` and its loop are gone. An ask with no card and no move line is `unfiled` until the server files it. A folded ask shows only in its card's thread; a moved ask leaves only its thread's link to the other board.
- **Retired.** `ask progress` and `ask done`, the `ask-done` and `ask-bump` events, the ask's Done and bump buttons, the snapshot's `askProgress` and `askTitles`, and `reply --title` on anything but a New or answered card. `question add` refuses an `item:ask:` topic, naming the card's topic. `ask move` refuses an ask whose card has started or been answered.
- **A deleted New card stays deleted.** Deleting a New card's message sends `ask-withdrawn` with the card's key; an `ask-withdrawn` counts as the ask's deletion whether or not it carries a key, so the dropped card is not filed again.
- **A waves board with no wave refuses the +.** Filing never makes a wave, and with no ask row the ask would have nowhere to show, so `fileAsk` fails with "this board has no wave yet to file a new task in; start a wave first".
