# Each answered question shows at once as sending

Superseded in part by [A question set's answers send as one](2026-11-16-2100-a-question-sets-answers-send-as-one.md): a Send is one outbox entry and one request, and closes the sheet.

Supersedes, for the questions sheet's Send, [Send records every ready answer in one request, all or none](2026-11-03-1519-send-records-every-ready-answer-in-one-request.md) (its one request and "No optimistic marking"), and replaces T-246's move-on once Send's POST succeeds. Extends [a thread message shows at once, and the browser keeps it until it is sent](2026-11-12-2030-a-thread-message-shows-at-once-and-the-browser-keeps-it-until-sent.md) and [every reply-box tap shows its line at once and retries in place](2026-11-14-0530-every-reply-box-tap-shows-its-line-at-once-and-retries-in-place.md) to answers.

The owner on T-234: "Also a separate task to pull this pattern into open questions for responsiveness". After the prototype (take A, sending… in the question's footer line): "Would like to push this further to be how all open questions are handled. And once all on the page are resolved only then does the topic move on."

- **Each question sends on its own.** Send puts each answered question in the browser's **answer outbox** (`app-questions-sheet.js`, kept under the board's own keys as `answer-outbox`, so a reload keeps it) and sends them one at a time, each its own `owner-answers` with one answer carrying a `sendId`. No button spins. The other questions in the sheet stay open and answerable meanwhile.
- **Its footer line says where it is.** A question in the outbox draws read-only, as a sent one does but with no Resolved badge, its sent line reading "<time> · sending…" in a thread footer's words (`ui-threads.js` `pickUp`), then "Sent." once the board has it. No dimming and no spinner (takes B and C dropped).
- **Not sent, Retry and X.** One the board can't be reached for reads "<time> · not sent" in red with Retry and X, holding no other answer back, since each answer stands alone. Retry sends it again under its `sendId`; X drops it and the question is open again with its pick and note, as its draft stays until the board has the answer. Neither asks a confirm: X loses nothing. One the board refuses (400) leaves the outbox with its question open again, and a toast says why.
- **The same line in the card's thread.** A card's question's answer draws in its thread as the board words it, "Question N · <question>\n→ <pick> · <note>", its footer "answers submitted · sending…" or not sent with Retry and X, the sheet's own (`app-threads.js` `answerChanged`). `threadOf` copies an answer's `sendId` onto its line, so the board's line takes the page's place; until a thread load brings it the line reads waiting…. A topic with no card has no thread, so only the sheet shows it.
- **The topic moves on once all are sent.** The sheet keeps the questions it opened with, sent ones included, and closes once the board has every question of its topic answered, back to the list. Waiting on you's row and the ? badge count only the open questions not in the outbox, so a topic whose every open question is on its way has no row.
- **A resend never doubles an answer.** The board records only answers to open questions (`store.mjs` `unrecorded`), so a resend after a lost answer records nothing new. Answers a reload left go out again, failed ones included, once the page first has its board; the board's sheet and Waiting on you's, both mounted on a phone, never send one at once.

One request per answer costs a POST and a board patch each, the trickle the 11/03 decision removed; each answer now shows its own state at once instead of waiting on the button, and the patches are small since boards sync by change number.

Out of scope: a question's Prototype request through the outbox (T-434), Reopen (T-435), and moving Send's follow-ups after the answer (T-245).
