# Status words are lowercase, and a board nothing will wake reads not listening

The owner, on #79's grill: every status word goes lowercase; sentences, toasts, tooltips and pushes keep their capital; a board that nothing will wake reads "not listening" everywhere (kept over "not responding", "stopped" and "deaf"); and no new "needs a restart" word. The listening words (not listening, asleep, waking…, listening) were already lowercase, and #131 and #135 already wrote implementing and starting that way. The words around them were capitalized.

- **A status word is lowercase.** A word that sits in a status slot (beside a status dot, in a `.st` span, in the board header's time slot, in a status chip with `role="status"`, or as a usage block's tag) reads lowercase: the sidebar's site words (`hub/home.mjs` `siteStatus`: down, starting, stopped, working, implementing, beside need you, waking… and asleep) and its paused; the hub status (`hubHealth`: healthy, degraded); the hub page's site rows (`hub/views.mjs` `siteHealth`: up, down · not answering, degraded · listening, stopped, starting), restarting, ready and not ready, Codex's not installed and not logged in, and Linear's doesn’t work; the board header's paused until and reconnecting…; a card's or main's building… and serving… chips and build failed and serve failed.
- **Everything else keeps its capital.** Sentences, toasts, tooltips and link details ("Asleep: its session idled out…", "Not listening: …"), pushes ("X isn't listening"), section, lane and row titles (the sidebar's pending Starting… and Cloning… rows), and button labels (Implement's Starting…, Merging…, main's Retry while it runs, Building… or Serving…, the hub's Restarting…) keep a capital first letter.
- **One word for a board nothing will wake.** It reads not listening in the header, sidebar and hub page, "Not listening: …" in the events link's detail and "X isn't listening" in its push. The code's names for that state (listenState `deaf`, `checks.watcher` `stopped`) aren't shown and stay.
- **No needs a restart.** The hub running older code than its files reads degraded with the detail "runs older code than its files; restart it", as before. A word added later for it comes in lowercase.

Considered: lowercasing sentences too ("asleep: its session idled out…", toasts and pushes starting lowercase). A sentence reads wrong without its capital, and the rule is easier to keep when it follows the slot, not the word.
