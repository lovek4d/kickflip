# Every signal that wakes the orchestrator says what to do

Card #395. Amends [record-only events do not wake the session](2026-11-05-1000-record-only-events-do-not-wake-the-session.md) and [only typed thread messages wake the orchestrator](2026-11-06-1100-only-typed-thread-messages-wake-the-orchestrator.md), and [staging, then ready](2026-11-07-0700-staging-then-ready.md) where a staging card goes ready.

The owner (#392, 11/06): "I think it's started to settle towards mostly being a mechanical task board. And the orchestrator really steps in and making sure an item that is marked as staging is prepared to move to ready … and to answer questions across questions … and to also be available for being grilled by or grilling the implementing agents or the grilling agents for that matter that might have questions before those questions surface to the user." And: "Really any signal that is reaching the orchestrator needs to have some auditing on whether it's properly represented in the skill and whether the skill is representing signals that the orchestrator doesn't have to worry about."

## Decision

- **One registry, kept by a test.** `bin/event-lines.mjs` holds `RECORD_ONLY` and the watcher's plain line for each kind (`PLAIN`, `ON_CARD`); `bin/wait-events.mjs` reads it. `bin/event-lines.test.mjs` scans the code's event writers (`appendEvent`, `appendEvents`, `sendToBoards`, the hub's board events and each page's `send`) and fails when a written kind is neither record-only nor lined, when a kind that wakes the session has no entry in SKILL.md, when SKILL.md describes as an event a kind the registry doesn't know, and when a wait kind in `board/domain.mjs` is missing from SKILL.md's Watch step (owner, gh395-guard). Each line says what to do, not only what came in.
- **`usage-resumed` is record-only** (owner, gh395-quiet). The watcher reads the pause gate itself and the work a pause held wakes the session once it lifts; the hub still resumes an asleep session on the line.
- **A bump on a card puts its waits first** (owner, gh395-bump). Bumped cards already top the waits list (`orchestratorWaits`); the `card-bump` line and SKILL.md now say to handle that card's waits before anything else and, with none, to write nothing.
- **A finished grill's questions wait on the orchestrator first** (owner, gh395-planner-asks, with the 15-minute publish of the recommendation). Questions a grill adds while it runs stay in `data/staged-questions.json`; when the grill's record closes, `data/screening.json` records the card and when, and the card gets a `screen` wait. The orchestrator runs `question settle <id> --note "<answer>"` for each question the board already answers, which publishes it resolved "Settled by the orchestrator: …" and writes the answer into the card's notes under "Answers (orchestrator, …)", as an owner's answer is written; then `question publish <key>` passes the rest on. The prototype question is the owner's and can't be settled. The board server publishes any left 15 minutes unscreened, with a thread line. A prototype planner's questions, a hold, a close and a hand-over publish at once, as before. A card with screened questions is never staged by an answer.
- **Staging to ready takes a judgment** (owner, gh395-staging). `item set --phase ready` on a staging card refuses without `--text "<what you checked>"`, and writes that line into the card's notes under "Ready (orchestrator, …)", since the move itself is a bare status line ([ready and staging moves are bare status lines](2026-11-08-0900-ready-and-staging-moves-are-bare-status-lines.md)). SKILL.md lists what to check against the whole board.
- **SKILL.md drops what no longer reaches the session.** The `card-merge` event (the Merge tap merges in the board server, [the Merge tap and auto-merge run in code](2026-11-04-2045-the-merge-tap-and-auto-merge-run-in-code.md)) and the wave band's `wave-triage` (#347 took Triage off the band; no page sends it). The waits list in step 5 names `staging`, `screen`, `intake` and `triage`.
- **A role paragraph heads the board section** of SKILL.md, in the owner's words.

## Not done

- `card-bump` still wakes for a card that hasn't started, whose bump the pull handles alone; the line says to write nothing then.
- The store still reads older `wave-triage` and `card-added` events; nothing new writes them.
- The overnight triage still leaves published questions the session could answer as they are.
