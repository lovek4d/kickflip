# Every reply-box tap shows its line at once and retries in place

Supersedes, for the buttons under a card's reply box, [a tap spins until the board shows its change](2026-11-05-0200-a-tap-spins-until-the-board-shows-its-change.md) and the "Only Implement waits" point of [a thread message shows at once, and the browser keeps it until it is sent](2026-11-12-2030-a-thread-message-shows-at-once-and-the-browser-keeps-it-until-sent.md).

The owner on #687: "Align all the thread reply type buttons with the normal reply tap. Let's workshop a sending status line", then "I'm thinking moving away from button spinners to instead retryable sending… in place", and after two visual rounds "Definitely D, but need a tidier retry without pills" and "Implemntation requested <dot> sending…/waiting… and then drop what's after the dot".

- **One outbox for every tap.** Implement, Grill, Prototype and Visual, and Hold (after its sheet), Resume, Done and Reopen (after their confirm), go through the outbox Reply uses (`app-threads.js` `enqueue`). An entry carries its event's kind and the rest of its data (`fields`, a Hold's `hold` and `for`); an entry with no kind is an `item-message`, so an outbox a reload kept from before reads as it did. No button spins: it is free again at once.
- **A bare tap is its status line.** It reads the board's words from the first instant, then a dot and where it is: "Prototype requested · 12:42 · sending…", then "· waiting…" until a session picks it up and "· thinking…" while it works; everything after the time drops once it is picked up. The time stays, as every status line keeps one ([every status line shows its time from one shell](2026-11-13-1959-every-status-line-shows-its-time-from-one-shell.md)). A request's event line (Grill, Prototype, Visual, re-ask) carries the state `threadOf` gives it, as a message does. Taps the board applies itself, Hold, Resume, Done, Reopen and a bare Implement, write lines no session picks up, so they end plain once the board has them.
- **A tap with text is the owner's message** with the tap in its footer, "12:42 · Implement · sending…" or "12:42 · Grill requested · sending…", then waiting…, thinking…, then the time and tap alone, as Reply reads.
- **Not sent** reads in red after the dot, then Retry and X as bare 16px ghost icons, the ones Reply's footer has. Retry resends under the same `sendId`; X drops it, a message's text going back in the box. A bare tap's confirm names its line ("Send “Resumed” again?").
- **The board's line takes the tap's place in one draw.** Once the board answers, the entry leaves the outbox and the thread shows it as landed (waiting… or plain) until a thread load started after the answer lands; that load's draw drops it and shows the board's line, so there is no gap and no duplicate.
- **A resend never doubles a tap.** The board server answers any event whose kind and `sendId` it already recorded with that event's id and records nothing (it did so only for `item-message`). A bare Implement records no event, so its resend after a lost answer is applied again, as a second Implement tap would be.
- **A Done from the box** closes its card once the board answers (`app-cards.js` `closeCard`), as before; a card tap's answer re-syncs the box's send buttons.

Out of scope: the buttons outside a card's reply box keep spinning as [a tap spins until the board shows its change](2026-11-05-0200-a-tap-spins-until-the-board-shows-its-change.md) has it: the action row's Hold, Close and Merge, the owner-step check, bump, lane Pull, review answers and Plan waves.

Considered: "Implement · sending…" for a bare tap's first instant, swapping to "Implementation requested" when the board has it (round 2's open point). The owner chose the requested words from the start, the same shape the footer of a tap with text already had.
