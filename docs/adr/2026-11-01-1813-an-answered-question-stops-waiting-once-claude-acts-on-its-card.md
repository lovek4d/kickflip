# An answered question stops waiting once Claude acts on its card

The owner found on hub/INT-217 that answered questions kept reading "waiting for Claude" after the session had acted on them: one question waits until `question resolve` runs, and sessions forget it. A confirm round made it worse, since it asks on a new prototype, so the round-1 question was neither replaced nor resolved. The board now resolves an answered question when the session does something that shows it acted on the answer. A question's states stay as `board/domain.mjs` `ownerQuestion` defines them.

- `board.mjs question add` on a card's topic (`item:<key>`) resolves every question on that card that is answered and still waiting on Claude, noting "Followed by <new id>", and prints `resolved: <id>` for each. A new question on a card means its earlier answers were acted on. Open questions on the card stay open, except where `question add` already replaces one on the same prototype ([the board serves every prototype from one template](2026-11-01-1448-the-board-serves-every-prototype-from-one-template.md)); an answered one there is resolved as followed, not replaced. Questions under a declared topic resolve nothing, since such a topic spans several cards.
- `board.mjs item set <key> --notes-file …` resolves the card's answered-and-waiting questions the same way, noting "Recorded in the card's notes", since a card's notes record what was settled ([notes replace the full plan, and a card starts on its own](2026-10-29-1816-notes-replace-the-full-plan-and-cards-start-on-their-own.md)).
- A question with a prototype url may list a subset of that prototype's takes as options, in take order, so a confirm round can show the picked take alone ([a prototype answer with a note gets a confirm round](2026-11-01-1514-a-prototype-answer-with-a-note-gets-a-confirm-round.md)). `question add` checks that the option ids are a non-empty, in-order subset of the recorded take keys; every take's file must still exist. The page still switches through every recorded take, and offers a pick only on the listed ones.
- A replied question, where Claude already answered the answer, is left as it is: it no longer waits on Claude.
