# Usage picks Opus, Auto or Sonnet for Claude builders, and builder pick says which and why

On #51 the owner asked that the builder's model be chosen in code, "The whole model selection should also go into the CLI or API", with Sonnet building simple changes. The owner picked take C of `gh51-claude-model`: a three-way Opus · Auto · Sonnet control at the top of the Claude part of the Hub page's Usage section, drawn like the Codex share control, with a helper line that always says what Auto does. It replaces the "Opus builders" on/off toggle of the earlier rounds (`gh51-allow-opus`, `gh51-allow-opus-usage`), which was never built.

- **The setting.** `claudeModel` in `~/.claude/kickflip/settings.json`, `opus`, `auto` or `sonnet`; missing or unknown reads Auto (`hub/push.mjs` `readClaudeModel`, `setClaudeModel`). The control posts `value` to `/_hub/settings/claude-model`, which answers `/_hub/api/hub`'s JSON, whose `claude` carries `{model, choices, description}` (`hub/views.mjs`), and redirects a plain form to `/_hub/`. The helpers read "Always Opus. Auto would give small cards to Sonnet.", "Auto: small cards get Sonnet, the rest get Opus." and "Always Sonnet. Auto would give large cards to Opus." The Claude block shows with the control even before Claude reports usage.
- **The card.** `item add` and `item set` take `--size small|large` and `--model opus|sonnet`, kept on the wave entry; `""` clears either. The session sets the size when it files or plans a card. Under Auto a card's `model` wins over its size; a small card gets Sonnet; any other card, sized large or not at all, gets Opus.
- **The pick.** `builder pick` keeps choosing Codex or Claude as before ([0131](0131-the-cli-and-board-server-do-the-boards-housekeeping.md)); for Claude it adds the model and why: "claude sonnet: Codex share is off. Claude model is Auto and the card is small." (`shared/usage-gate.mjs` `builderPick`). A build's `builder start` prints the same reason on its `kind:` line and the model on its `model:` line, and records the model. The owner's Build tap records it too, and `card-build` carries `model` for a Claude build ([0139](0139-a-build-tap-starts-the-build-in-the-board-server.md)).
- **The refusal.** While the setting is Opus or Sonnet, `builder start --kind claude|build --model <other>` (or a legacy `--kind opus|sonnet`) is refused, naming the setting. Under Auto `--model` is free, as the session's call on that one build. Prototype and grill agents keep their own models (#111); the setting is for builds only.
- **Who takes over.** The Codex share descriptions and the pause line's helper name who builds when Codex doesn't: Opus or Sonnet when the setting forces one, Claude under Auto ("Codex builds and reviews, and Claude takes over while it's out.", "Codex hands its work to Sonnet builders.").
- **Shows.** The pick's choice and reason show only in the CLI prints (`gh51-shows-proto`, take C); the thread's agent line already names the model ("Claude Sonnet builder started"). Logging and auditing the picks is its own card (#121).

Considered:
- an "Allow Opus builders" toggle, off sending every build to Sonnet: the owner's third round replaced it with the three-way control, so Opus can be forced too;
- the session judging each card's model from a board rule: sessions forget rules, and the owner wanted the choice in code;
- refusing an Auto `--model` that differs from the card's pick: the owner's override is the card's `--model`, and refusing the builder's own pass would leave no way to run a one-off without editing the card.
