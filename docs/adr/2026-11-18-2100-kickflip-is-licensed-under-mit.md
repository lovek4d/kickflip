# Kickflip is licensed under MIT

The owner picked MIT on hub/T-718, replacing [Prosperity 3.0.0](2026-11-01-1353-kickflip-is-licensed-under-prosperity-3-0-0.md), which is not open source: it allowed only noncommercial use and a 30-day commercial trial.

- `LICENSE` is the standard MIT text, "Copyright (c) 2026 Love Kharod". `package.json` `license` is `MIT`.
- MIT already allows commercial use, so the README's trial, contact and Ever lines are gone.
- The change applies from the next release. Versions already published on npm keep Prosperity.
- Apache 2.0, GPL 3.0 and AGPL 3.0 were the other options. MIT won on simplicity and adoption among npm packages.
