# A question's takes show inline, and back goes one step

Superseded in part by [a question's prototypes open in a full-page viewer](2026-11-05-1302-a-questions-prototypes-open-in-a-full-page-viewer.md): Waiting on you frames no take, the two layouts, frame measuring and `?take=` are gone, and a pick on a prototype page drafts it for the question's Send instead of sending at once. Diagrams, back and the shared header stand.

Superseded in part by [one app for the whole hub](2026-10-29-2005-one-app-for-the-whole-hub.md) on how back finds the step: in the app shell the referrer stops changing, so each history entry carries `{idx}`, and the arrow goes back while `idx` is above 0. A page a push opened still goes to its `backHref`.

Superseded in part by [Back follows the page tree](2026-10-30-1125-back-follows-the-page-tree.md) on back from Waiting on you and a prototype page: in the app it goes up a level, from a prototype to Waiting on you on its question and from there to the board, however the owner arrived, a push included. Every other page still goes one step back.

A question's takes show on the questions page itself, not behind a link. Every page opens in the same window, and its back arrow goes one step back.

- **Two layouts keep takes on Waiting on you.** Options stay in stored take order, with `recommended: true` shown as a Recommended tag. If every prototype take fits within 80% of the screen's height at its grid width, takes share a grid with columns at least 380px wide, one column on a phone, each option above its frame. If any take exceeds that height, or is a full-screen page, the whole question shows its existing radio options as a choice row above one full-width frame. Questions with diagram or practice options keep their existing layout.
- **A full-screen take is always full size.** The chosen take fills the page's content width under the same Waiting on you header and question pills. Before a choice, the recommended take shows, or the first when none is recommended; its option is outlined, and displaying it does not draft an answer. A draft or last answer chooses its take. The note and the page's Send follow the frame. Changing the option switches the frame and returns to its top, without navigation, a separate pill, or an Open full size link.
- **A frame fits its page.** The frame takes its page's own measured height at its displayed width, so Waiting on you scrolls through it. A page whose body is exactly as tall as its frame is measured again in a taller frame: one that follows the frame fills the screen, as an app-shell prototype does, so it is a full-screen page, and its frame gets the screen's height between the header and the Send bar. Each take loads for measurement, including takes outside the viewport; grid measurements choose the layout, and a separate measurement sizes the chosen take at full width. The page is measured again as it grows or the width changes, and stays hidden with “Loading take…” until measured. At `?take=` `prototype-switcher.js` hides the page's header and pill and trims the bottom padding kept clear for the pill.
- **What a take shows comes from the question.** A question whose `url` is a prototype page already says each option is one of that page's takes ([prototypes come only when obviously helpful or on request](2026-10-29-1639-prototypes-come-on-request.md)), so it needs no new field. Each option then shows the page in a frame at `?take=<option id>`, where `prototype-switcher.js` shows that take alone, with no header or pill. An option with nothing to see carries `diagram`, rows of up to four boxes `{text, tone?, to?}` coloured by the status colours, and `practice`, a line or two of what it means in practice. The page draws them as boxes and SVG arrows that wrap at any width. `board.mjs question add` checks both fields. A question whose takes show in frames drops its "See the prototypes →" pill; one drawn as diagrams keeps it.
- **A prototype opened separately keeps its controls.** A thread link opens the prototype page in the same window, with its own header and ‹ take › pill at `?variant=<option id>`. Links to pages on the hub open in place everywhere on the board; only another origin opens a new window.
- **Back goes one step.** Every page header with a back arrow (`ui-page-header.js`) goes one step back in history when the page before was on the hub. When a push opened the page, or nothing came before it, the arrow goes to its `backHref`, the work item or the board. `store.mjs pushUrl` marks every push to a page other than the board with `from=push`. The header keeps that mark in `sessionStorage` for the page's reloads, since the prototype page drops it from its URL ([a push lands where the owner answers](2026-10-29-1639-a-push-lands-where-the-owner-answers.md)).
- **Prototype pages share the board header**, `<!--header {"lead":"back","backHref":"./#item=<wave>%2F<id>","title":"…","controls":false}-->`. The back arrow stays at the left, in the bottom bar below 900px ([phone headers sit at the bottom, with boards nearest the thumb](2026-10-31-1532-phone-headers-sit-at-the-bottom.md)) and at the top on desktop.
- **A pick on a prototype page sends at once.** Pick this has no confirmation, and a later pick changes it. A note box above the pill goes with it, and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter in it sends. The page then uses `BoardUI.nextQuestion` in `ui-questions.js`, the same pure move-on rule as Send on Waiting on you ([Waiting on you leads with what needs the owner](2026-10-29-1639-waiting-on-you-leads-with-what-needs-the-owner.md)), and opens the returned question’s prototype page, or its card on `questions.html`. When it returns null, the pick leaves `BoardUI.sentNotice` under the board’s route and goes to the board, whose Sent toast says what it sent. Framed in the app shell, the pick uses the top window’s `BoardUI`, which owns that notice. Opened alone, the switcher loads `ui.js`, `ui-links.js` and `ui-questions.js` if needed, so prototype pages need only their existing scripts.

The owner settled the two layouts on `full-size`: full-screen prototypes always show full size below a top-level choice, while small screenshot-style prototypes can share a grid. This keeps pending questions and their controls in view without a second page.

Earlier choices, superseded by the two layouts above where they differ:

The owner asked on INT-104: "Prototypes should not spawn in a new window, and back should not link all the way back to the task; back should go back in nav one step. If a prototype was opened from a push notification, only then should back become back to work item. Also prototypes should share the header with the rest. Even better, prototypes should be shown inline with the grill page, instead of a regular multiple choice." From its prototypes they chose:
- `pi-layout` carousel: one take at a time with the next peeking, side by side on a wide screen. On INT-150 they found side by side cramped and asked to stack the takes, with a take that scrolls going straight to full size, and picked `takes-stack` fit: each frame sized to its page. They then asked for a grid where takes fit side by side, so a wide screen shows more than one per row;
- `pi-open` page: full size opens as a page in the same window, and back goes one step;
- `pi-diagram` svg: boxes and arrows drawn to fit, coloured by meaning, with what the take means in practice.

While it was built, they added a pick with no confirmation, a note with the pick, and moving on to the next open question after one.

Considered:
- the option list with each preview opening under its option (`pi-layout` expand);
- stacked frames of a fixed height, a take that scrolls showing the button or its top fading out under Open full size (`takes-stack` link, peek);
- full size as a sheet over the questions page, or no full size at all (`pi-open` sheet, inline);
- a take with nothing to see as numbered steps, or as a text diagram that scrolls sideways on a phone (`pi-diagram` steps, ascii);
- a per-option `preview` url, which repeats the question's own prototype page on every option.
