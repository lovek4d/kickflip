# ↗ marks only links that leave the page

Status: accepted (#259)

A link that opens the reader sheet over the board (a thread's "Open the log", a card's or lineup's Notes) carries no mark: it stays on the page. ↗ (LINK_ARROW) stays on links that leave it: GitHub, Open alone, Open in VS Code, Visit and Install. Modal openers elsewhere (settings, architecture, sheets) also carry no mark.

The audit that led here (every link and what it opens) is in the card's notes. Prototypes and Open questions stay full pages and keep their current marks for now.
