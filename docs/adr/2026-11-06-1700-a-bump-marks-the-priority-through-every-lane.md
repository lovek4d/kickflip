# A bump marks the priority through every lane

Card #327. A bump counted only in Todo and In Progress: one made before the first build cleared when that build started, and In Review dropped it. The owner (11/05): "Keep bump tied to a work item. So when it moves to in progress, it maintains its bumped status. This is effectively saying a given item is a priority, and it should push through all lanes as first priority." Answers on the card (11/06) settled the rest; a paused-build state was prototyped and dropped for a bump-out (gh327-look), and the bumped-out card's look is take B of gh327-bump-out-3b.

## Decision

- **A bump lasts.** It counts from its `card-bump` until a later `card-unbump` or the card's close, merged or Done (gh327-clear). A bump made before the card last closed is over, so a reopened card needs a new one. Every open work card carries the bump icon, In Review included; a card whose code landed and waits on its owner step has closed, and its Done sits where the bump would.
- **Earliest bump first.** Every lane but Done lists its bumped cards first, the earliest bump on top (gh327-order). A bump again moves the card behind the earlier bumps, so a bumped New card's bump icon is disabled.
- **Every stage takes bumped cards first.** The pull, planners, landers and rebase runs take bumped cards first, and the orchestrator's wait list (`orchestrator-waits.json`, the watcher's `waits` lines) lists bumped cards' waits first across lanes, each `waits` line marked `bumped` (gh327-waits).
- **Past the cap.** A bumped ready card starts at once whatever Maximum parallel implementers leaves (`domain.mjs pullOf`), and a bumped arrival gets its grill whatever Maximum parallel planners leaves (`arrivalsOf`) (gh327-cap). Landers and rebase runs count against the cap and now launch only into a free slot, before the helpers and the pull, a bumped card's past the cap (the owner's note on gh327-cap: "Any in progress item should just not start any new phases past the cap").
- **A bump-out, not a pause.** Each bumped pull past the cap bumps out the newest unbumped build (gh327-lowest, the owner's note on gh327-look): the board stops its headless builder, marked stopped so nothing relaunches it, and sends the card back to Todo as ready, keeping its worktree, branch and thread (`stop.mjs bumpOut`, `store.mjs sendBackBumpedOut`). The thread reads "Bumped out by #61: back to Todo, ready. It starts again once a slot frees." and its footer status "ready · bumped out by #61" in grey until it starts again (gh327-bump-out-3b, take B). Todo lists bumped-out cards right after the bumps, so the pull takes one next once a slot frees. Only an In Progress card with no PR, pushed branch or held Merge, whose one run the board launched and with no helper open, not landing or checking, is bumped out: a session's own builder can't be stopped by the board, and the rest would lose more work. With none to bump out the bump starts anyway. Only a pull bumps out a build; a bumped lander or rebase run past the cap stops nothing.
- **A busy host holds a bump too** (gh327-hold, gh327-usage). A busy host holds all new work, bumps included, and a bumped ready card reads "held while host recovers" in grey while it does. A usage pause holds a bump like everything else, with no word of its own. User-facing words say host, never Mac.

## Considered

- Pausing the lowest-priority build in place, the card reading "Paused for #N" with a grey paused footer, and relaunching it first (gh327-pause): dropped by the owner for the bump-out, which reuses Todo, ready and the pull.
- A bump-out with no words on the card (take A) and one that also marks the winner "Started past the cap, bumped out #N" (take C).
- Stale and stalled were split to their own card (#378).

Superseded in part by [a card pause stops its builder at its next stopping point](2026-11-09-1300-a-card-pause-stops-its-builder-at-its-next-stopping-point.md): the bump-out pauses the newest unbumped build In Progress instead of sending it back to Todo.
