The slot where Done's check sat holds one bare orange glyph for the owner's next, icon only at every width. It never holds a `?` for open questions (owner, 11/24):

- Implement while the orchestrator's offer is open (the card's action is Tap Implement), not on every Todo card; it confirms, then sends the build, as the thread's Implement does.
- Done's check for a card whose action is Tap Done, or that waits on an owner step or a published release, as before.

The slot follows the card's action, as the action-needed flag does: an action kind the slot can take in one tap (Implement, Done) shows its glyph, every other kind shows none. Open questions take no slot: the card's questions button and Next line carry them (owner, 11/24).

There is no Close: an answered ask's next is Done. Every glyph is orange, whatever the lane.
