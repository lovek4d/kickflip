# A card's top right offers the owner's next in one tap

The slot where Done's check sat holds one bare orange glyph for the owner's next, icon only at every width:

- `?` while the card has open questions; it opens the questions sheet. Questions replace any other next, with no count and no pip, and the Next line is unchanged.
- Implement while the orchestrator's offer is open (the card's action is Tap Implement), not on every Todo card; it confirms, then sends the build, as the thread's Implement does.
- Done's check for a card waiting on an owner step or a published release, as before.

There is no Close: an answered ask's next is Done. Every glyph is orange, whatever the lane, and the `?` has no circle around it (owner, 11/24).
