# A card is grilled through its own open questions

When the owner asks for a grill on work, Claude makes the card first. Each grill question is then an owner question on that card (`board.mjs question add`, topic `item:<wave>/<id>`): lettered options with the recommended one first, context, and a prototype page in `url` when one helps. The questions show in Needs you and on the open card, and the owner answers each one there. A prototype is always such a question: one option per take, ids matching the take keys, and `url` at `prototype-<topic>.html?q=<question id>`, whose takes questions.html shows inline and whose switcher can send the pick ([0048](0048-a-questions-takes-show-inline.md)) ([0015](0015-prototypes-come-on-request.md)). Questions that span several cards go under a declared topic (`topic add`), never a grill round.

There are no grill rounds ([0041](0041-hub-changes-come-as-asks-not-improvement-rounds.md)). A card's thread never carries a bare link to a grill page instead of the questions.

A wave's planning grill follows the same rule. Planning is Claude's internal work first: it drafts the items as planned cards before asking the owner anything, then asks on the cards. When a wave finishes, the next one comes the same way: a "Plan the next wave" card, drafted from the finished wave's follow-ups and pending work, with its questions on the card, and nothing builds until the owner answers them (round `site-v18`, question v18-next-wave, option A; considered: a line in Needs you, or starting at once any PR an earlier grill already settled). When the board's question view lacks something a grill needs, Claude extends the shared board pages rather than building a page for one wave or card.

The owner chose this after a grill on a `shipyard-orchestrator` card went up as a grill round with a link from the card's thread (2026-10-23): "This should have used open items to grill, not a bare link." On the planning question that followed, they chose cards first, noting: "Would rather expand the shared grill page as needed. Planning should be an internal task first."
