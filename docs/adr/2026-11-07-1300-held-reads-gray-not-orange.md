# Held reads gray, not orange

A card's footer "held" / "held until …" (`.card-hold`) is drawn in `--color-quiet`, the gray of idle and muted text, split out of the orange `--color-notice` it shared with `.card-blocked` and `.card-action` (#374). The owner asked for it ("Held should be gray not orange"). The Unhold button stays orange; no prototype round, by the owner's answer.
