# A card pause stops its builder at its next stopping point

Card #432. A blocked card went back to Todo with its builder killed, a held card's builder was killed at once, a bump-out sent the newest build back to Todo, and the pause line let running work run on until the builder hit the hard usage limit. Each threw away the builder's context, and a card that had started read as unstarted. The owner's answers on the card (11/09) replace all four with one mechanism.

**One card pause.** A blocked, held, usage-paused (the pause line, Pause board and Pause hub; a builder at the hard limit takes the same paused state) or bumped-out card's open main build gets a `pause {cause, words, at, by?, until?}` on its builder record, and the card stays In Progress with its worktree, branch and record. A busy host is not a cause: running work still finishes there. A landing card is never paused. `store.mjs` `requestPause` writes it; `domain.mjs` `pauseWork` decides each tick which pauses to ask (the usage pause), drop, force or resume.

**Stopping point.** The builder's next progress report, call or ask is its stopping point: the reply tells it to commit what it has and end its turn, and the board marks the build paused, writing "Paused at <phase>: blocked by #53. It resumes once nothing blocks it." A builder in `builder asks --wait` is already at one and gets the same reply. Past 15 minutes (`PAUSE_HARD_STOP`) the board marks it paused and stops its headless process. A cause that clears before the builder stops drops the pause with no line. A paused build frees its implementer slot.

**Resume.** Once nothing pauses it the board writes "Resumed: nothing blocks it." (or "unheld", "the usage pause lifted", "a slot freed") and resumes the builder's own Claude session, `claude --resume <session>`, in its worktree, telling it the pause cleared and its record is still open (`headless.mjs` `resumeBuilder`; a Codex or session-less build launches fresh). Paused cards resume before the pull starts new ones, oldest pause first, as slots allow. A builder running in a session the board didn't launch gets the same pause reply, and the clear sends `builder-resume` for the orchestrator to tell it to carry on.

**Words.** The card's footer reads "pausing · <cause>" in orange (work under way, the colour rule's orange), then "paused · <cause>" in grey, in place of held or blocked by; its phase bars stay at the phase it stopped at, dimmed (take A). The usage cause reads "usage limit", or "board paused" / "hub paused" for the owner's manual pauses. The Todo "bumped out by #N" footer and its sort are gone.
