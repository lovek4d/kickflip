# A wave row's counts take their lanes' colours; the Triage button and "N of M merged" go

A wave header's own lane number, the bold one in x/y/k/z, takes that lane's colour: In Review blue, In Progress orange, Todo and Done gray (it was always the accent blue).

The Triage button, its "triaging…" and "Triage: N open questions" lines are gone from the header. Triage is asked through New task. The overnight triage and the `wave-triage` event are untouched.

"N of M merged" is gone from the header, since the four counts on the right already say where the wave's cards are. The started cards ("P2 in progress") stay.
