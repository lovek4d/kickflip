# The header's refresh always shows the data's age

The header's time beside refresh reads the age of the data ("2 min") in every state. It no longer swaps to "not listening", "paused until …", "reconnecting…", "asleep" or "waking…".

The state moves only the colour and the spin: a stopped watcher or an offline link turns the time and the arrow red, paused usage turns them orange (red wins when both hold), and a refresh tap that is reloading or reconnecting spins the icon. Everything else is the normal text colour.

The old words live on as the time's title, ahead of the "Updated … ago" line. Asleep and waking show no colour change; only the title says so.
