# A board is shared view only with a Tailscale login

Since #210 the hub answers only the owner (docs/adr/2026-11-04-2156-the-whole-hub-answers-only-the-owner.md). The owner asked for a board-level setting naming another Tailscale identity that can reach the board (#242, board-share-notes.md), and answered the grill and prototype round 1 (take A).

- **The list lives in board.json `share`, as Tailscale sends it.** Each entry is a login exactly as tailscale serve's `Tailscale-User-Login` sends it: an email, or `name@github` for a GitHub sign-in. Board settings writes it through the board server's `POST /api/share {add | remove}` (store.mjs saveBoardShare), beside the other board.json writes, and wakes no session. The hub reads board.json on each request from a login that isn't the owner's, since Board settings edits it underneath.
- **View only (owner, gh242-can-do: A).** `hub/share.mjs shareReach` decides what a shared login reaches: the shell and its files, `/_hub/sidebar.json` and `/_hub/stream`, and, by GET or HEAD, an allow-list of its shared boards' paths: the board's pages and the files they load, its prototypes and uploads, and its data, threads, notes and Main's build log. No board GET marks anything read, so refusing every other method at the hub keeps it view only. The check runs on the hub's one WHATWG parse of the path, and the hub forwards a shared person's request as exactly that path, since the board strips its own `/<route>` again and parses a leading `//` as a host: forwarding the raw URL let `/orch/orch/api/log` or `/orch//x/../api/log` reach the host's logs. A write, and any other board path (Board settings and the reads behind it, among them `/api/log`, which reads any host log by name; the event log; the board's own `/api/stream`, since the shell follows `/_hub/stream`; and upgrades), get front-door.mjs's `view-only` refusal: 403, "You can look at this board, not change it." in a browser, `{error}` otherwise. Anything else is `other`, the #210 refusal. Fuller permissions are #295.
- **The shell with only their boards (gh242-hub-view: A; gh242-look: A).** `sharedSidebar` gives a shared person their boards alone, without the owner's waiting counts, context readings, usage, hub row, notifications or starting boards, both in the shell's first paint and on the stream; the stream passes them only their boards' changes. Removing a share ends that person's open `/_hub/stream` within 2 seconds, at once after Board settings' POST, and its EventSource comes back through the gate. The sidebar drops the Hub row and New board, so on a phone the header logo opens a drawer of just their boards. The hub's own pages redirect them to `/`. shell.js marks the page `.shared-view`, which hides the +, every composer, the gear, the bulb and a card's buttons, keeps its links, opens the board with a "View only." strip naming the owner by Tailscale display name, and sends no presence beats.
- **Presence and pushes stay the owner's.** `/_hub/presence` and `/_hub/push/*` are refused to a shared login, so its taps never count as the owner here and it never subscribes to pushes.
- **Visit links open (gh242-try: A).** A try relay admits its board's shared logins by GET or HEAD; another method, or an upgrade, gets the view-only refusal, and removing a share ends that login's open relay responses on the same 2-second check as `/_hub/stream`.
- **Sharing sits in Board settings under Reviewers (gh242-add, gh242-reach, gh242-viewing, gh242-look: A).** Each person is a row with their name, then their login and "viewing now", "x min ago" or "not seen yet"; each login refused in the last hour at that board's paths or at a path naming no board is a row the same shape with Add (owner: "match the format  on pending rows, should be dry: new line for the id and x min ago"); a field adds a typed login; one line says they also need the host shared to them in Tailscale, linking its Machines page, with Copy link. The hub answers `GET /_hub/share/<route>` with these, from `shareTracker`: viewing now is an open stream whose last board is this one, last seen is kept per board in `share-seen.json` and written at most once a minute, and the refused list lives in memory. The link is the board's URL on the host's MagicDNS name, read from `tailscale status`.
- **Not verified live.** What `Tailscale-User-Login` a user from another tailnet sends once the host is shared to them was not checked, since no second tailnet was at hand. The login is kept exactly as sent, and Add on a just-refused row copies that header, so it matches whatever Tailscale sends.
