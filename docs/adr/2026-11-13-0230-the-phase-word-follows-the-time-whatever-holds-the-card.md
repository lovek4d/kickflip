# The phase word follows the time, whatever holds the card

The footer showed a card's phase word only when no hold or block word was there, and a ready card that something blocked showed no ready word ([every lane has phases](2026-11-11-1200-every-lane-has-phases.md), [two rows under a card](2026-11-12-0630-action-needed-and-new-messages-get-their-own-row.md)). The owner (card #627) wanted every phase word to stay.

- **Order.** Time, then the phase word, then held, then blocked by: "11/12 · ready · held", "11/12 · staging · blocked by #61".
- **Every phase word.** discussing, grilling, prototyping, staging, ready and the In Review words all show. In Progress and New cards show none, as before.
- **Colour.** Unchanged: each word keeps its own tone.

This amends both ADRs above where they say the phase word shows only when neither a hold nor a block does, and that a blocked ready card shows no ready.
