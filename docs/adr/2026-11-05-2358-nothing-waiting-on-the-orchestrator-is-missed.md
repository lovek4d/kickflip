# Nothing waiting on the orchestrator is missed: a wait re-wakes it, and a block can wait on a condition

#300. Three cards (#223, #263, #266) sat unverified for up to 6.5 hours: each wait's `orchestrator-waits {added}` event fired once, and the orchestrator read the watcher's output through a filter that dropped the `waits` lines, the only place the card showed after that. The fix moves out of skill language into the board server.

The owner's answers (11/05–11/06): re-wake every 15 minutes until picked up (gh300-rebump A), and hourly while a picked-up wait stays open (gh300-picked A); wake only for a wait the board can't move itself (gh300-valid); a block widens to a card or a free-text condition an agent checks, with a reason, not a time ("that is held territory"), set and cleared through `item set` by the owner and the orchestrator; clearing it wakes the orchestrator once; it reads "blocked until <condition>, reason". The Sonnet triage agent and the orchestrator's model went to #317; what a block does past Todo went to #362.

- **A wait re-wakes the orchestrator until it is handled.** `domain.mjs` `waitsStep` runs on each wait check (every board change and each minute, `server.mjs` `stepWaits`). Each entry in `data/orchestrator-waits.json` keeps `bumped`, its last wake, so a restart keeps the cadence. A wait not picked up wakes again `REWAKE_AFTER` (15 minutes) after its last wake; a picked-up one `PICKED_REWAKE_AFTER` (an hour) after the later of its pickup and its last wake, or a day for a stale review ([the orchestrator reviews every lane on a schedule](2026-11-06-1653-the-orchestrator-reviews-every-lane-on-a-schedule.md)). One check appends at most one `orchestrator-waits {added: [key], again: [{key, ref, kind, since, next}]}`, so several due cards ride one event and a usage pause can't stack them. The watcher's plain line names them: "still waiting on you: #263 calls for 2h: Verify the builder's judgment calls" (`waitsAgainLine`), so reading only event lines misses no wait. An entry stored before `bumped` counts from its `since`, so the first check after the upgrade wakes once for every old wait.
- **Only waits the board can't move itself wake it.** Reading the code for each wait kind: the full In Progress cap never makes a wait (a ready card waiting for a slot is the pull's), a grill or prototype request launches its planner at the tap, and a build's record opens `started` at the board's own start, so there is no unowned gap between a pull and its launch. The one wait the board resolves itself is a builder whose launch a usage pause held (`headless-builders.json` pid null, `handOff`), which `reviveHeadless` launches once the pause lifts. The snapshot's `alive` reads `'held'` for it, and `builderOf` reads it held and never stalled, so it is no `stalled` wait. The `answer` gap (an answer recorded, the card not yet ready) stays the orchestrator's until #297's staging moves it into code.
- **A block waits on cards or on a condition, with a reason.** `item set --blocked-until "<condition>"` stores `blocked_until`, `--block-reason "<reason>"` stores `block_reason` for either kind of block, and a reason with no block is refused; the reason goes when the block does. A card with a condition is blocked as a dep blocks it (`buildBoard` `blocked`): not ready, not pulled, not grilled on arrival, its `--status building` refused, and the Build tap bumps it. A finishing dep doesn't unblock a card its condition still holds. `--blocked-until ""` on an open card nothing else blocks threads "Unblocked: <condition>." and appends `card-unblocked {key}` once, on any open card; a wait still true shows again through the list. The time line reads "blocked until <condition>, <reason>", after any deps "blocked by #53 and until <condition>, <reason>", in the orange of the blocked words (`ui-cards.js` `blockedWords`). The condition has no automatic check: the orchestrator checks it on its passes over the cards, the walk over every lane at the hub's morning wake ([the orchestrator reviews every lane on a schedule](2026-11-06-1653-the-orchestrator-reviews-every-lane-on-a-schedule.md)).
- **Past Todo nothing changes.** A block on a started card still only refuses `--status building`; whether it should silence wakes, stop phase changes or landing, or bump the card back to Todo is #362's.

Considered:
- a fixed re-wake for every wait without a board-can-move check: it pings for gaps the board closes itself, which the owner ruled out;
- `orchestrator-waits` as a held usage-gate kind, so re-wakes wait for a pause to end: a builder's ask and a calls hand-back still need the orchestrator during a pause;
- a time as a block's condition: a hold with `--pick-up-at` already is one.
