# A requested brief is owed in every lane

A brief requested (typed /grill, /prototype, /visual or /review, or the request row) and not yet written is a wait on the orchestrator in every lane, Done included. Before, a grill, prototype or visual brief was owed only in Todo and a reviewer's in any open lane, so /visual on an In Progress card never woke the orchestrator.

The rule is one for every kind, with no lane check. A brief is still written only on demand, never automatically. A card outside Todo, In Progress and In Review (Done) owes that wait alone.
