# The Needs you button reaches the prototypes, and the Prototypes button is dropped

Supersedes the Questions and Prototypes buttons of [prototypes have their own button and a full-bleed viewer](2026-11-07-0300-prototypes-have-their-own-button-and-viewer.md); its viewer, rounds and bar stand.

The owner on #416: "lets drop the prototypes button, can access them through questions button, which should be 'Needs you' instead of 'Questions'".

- **One button.** The card's action row reads Hold, Notes, Serve, **Needs you**, Merge. Needs you is the old Questions button under its new name: it opens the card's questions page on its first question, solid orange while one waits on the owner, plain otherwise.
- **The prototypes stay reachable.** Every prototype round is a question on its card, so a card with prototypes always has the button, Done cards included, and the questions page shows each prototype question's View prototypes link to the viewer. No card loses its way in; the board needed no new rule for Done cards with old rounds.
- **The card's rounds.** `prototypes` on the card stays: the viewer's Round pill reads it. Only the button that used it is gone.
