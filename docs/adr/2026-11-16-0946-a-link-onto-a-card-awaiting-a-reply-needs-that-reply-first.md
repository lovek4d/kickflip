# A link onto a card awaiting a reply needs that reply first

The owner asked on a card, and the orchestrator filed a task from it before answering: the new task's "Related to" line landed on that card 17 s before the reply, and named a task the owner couldn't open yet, since it was still in New. The orchestrator's reply on another card had also named a T-number before `item add` made it. The rule that the message comes before the status line is now mechanical.

- **Reply before link.** `item add` (with or without `--ask`), `item triage` and `item set` with `--parent`, `--relate`, `--related`, `--unrelate`, `--blocked-by` or `--blocks` refuse when a card they link or unlink waits on the orchestrator's reply to the owner, naming the card and `--reply-on "<text>"`. With `--reply-on`, the board writes that text on the card as the orchestrator's message, then the link's line, in the same command. `--reply-on` with no waiting card is refused, since it would write nowhere; with two waiting cards it is refused too, as one text can't answer both. Triage also checks the cards its held Related to lines go to (below).
- **What waits.** A card waits while the board server's list (`data/orchestrator-waits.json`) holds a `reply` wait for it, the owner's word last on the card (`domain.mjs ownerWordOf`), and no plain message of the orchestrator's on the card, nor a reply to one of the owner's messages there, came since. The list is recomputed on every board change, so the command's own check only covers the moment after the orchestrator answers and before the list catches up. A message the owner sent in the second before the list catches up is not yet seen; that link goes through as before.
- **Related to waits for triage.** A task filed in New (`item add --ask --relate`) writes its own "Related to" line at once, but the line on the other card waits until the task leaves New, which only triage (or the Quick build's `item set --phase ready --from-ask`) does, since every other write to a New card is refused. Then each related card without a standing Related to line for it gets one. Parent ("Created ticket") and block lines are written at once as before.
- **No unborn names.** A `thread` line, `reply`, `--text`, `--ask` or `--reply-on` naming `T-<n>` at or past the board's next task number, bare or keyed by one of its item files, is refused: "T-<n> doesn't exist yet: file it first and name the key item add printed". Quoted words are someone else's and don't count.
- **A New link opens New.** A task link to a card still in New (an ask not yet triaged) scrolls to its row in the New strip and tints it, instead of opening a card that no lane draws. The link's text and address are unchanged, so every link (thread text, link lines, footers) behaves the same, and once triaged it opens the card.
- **The orchestrator's ask reads as its own.** An ask with `data.asked` (one the orchestrator, a builder or a planner filed) draws as the orchestrator's message (`who: claude`) under its From line, not as the owner's blue bubble. Its words still approve nothing: the owner-quote gates skip `data.asked` as before.
